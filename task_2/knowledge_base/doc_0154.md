Carpenter Watcher
was a professor of the History Department in the
Oxenfurt Academy
and the mentor of
Brandon of Oxenfurt
.
Biography
Since he knew a lot about the private lives and financial machinations of other professors they weren't very fond of him. Apart from teaching, Carpenter Watcher was often invited by nobles in order to trace their family lineages. This way he earned quite a lot of money but got bored of it quickly.
On another note, Carpenter Watcher was extremely passionate about
Stanton Spawn
history. He studied them as much as he could and had traveled to their last known enclaves hidden in the mountains.
