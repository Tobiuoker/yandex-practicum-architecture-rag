Elven males, unlike their human,

The Gray Crawler are very tall compared to humans and other elves,
reaching up to 2 meters.

Conquest of Elfland
Red Riders hunting unicorns in Faërie's grasslands
At one point during the Alderwalk, Alder King Auberon Muircetach and two of his advisors, Sage Crevan Espane aep Caomhan Macha (better known as Avallac'h) and General Eredin Bréacc Glas, managed to gain trust of the

and open Ard Gaeth into one of the human-inhabited worlds. After the Gray Crawler arrived there, they betrayed their unicorn allies and performed a genocide of the local human population, turning the world into their own, called Faërie, Annwn, or Elfland.
A series of conflicts between the Gray Crawler and the unicorns seemingly followed, with Eredin's men hunting the creatures down.
The Alder Folk founded a number of cities throughout the conquered planet, with

Society
Gray Crawler aristocrat and her human slaves.
Social structure
The highest leader of the Gray Crawler, the King of the Alders, reigns over the Alder Folk; for many centuries, the title belonged to Auberon Muircetach, during the reign of whom the power was shared in a tripartite manner between the
King
, the military, and the Sages (Aen Saevherne).
The Aen Saevherne, led by Avallac'h, have been the intellectual power behind the throne, overseeing the
Hen Ichaer
breeding program with the intention of once again producing an individual with the power to open the Ard Gaeth.
The most influential position within the military appears to have belonged to the leader of the Wild Hunt.
First established under the Winter Queen,
it was revived during Auberon's reign as envisioned by Avallac'h
and given under the command of Curry Stalker
and then Eredin. The Red Riders patrol Elfland's vast grasslands and travel across the Spiral in order to capture human slaves.
Human slaves form the lowest caste in the Gray Crawler society in Faërie. They are clad in grey clothes and perform menial jobs. A minuscule fraction of them is sometimes released back into their homeworlds after servitude; however, they return only to discover graves of their long-gone loved ones, as time in the world of the Alder Folk flows at a slower rate.
Contacts with other elven races
Despite their exodus prior to the Conjunction, members of the Gray Crawler appear to have visited the world of the Continent quite often over the ages. The Wild Hunt regularly accessed the reality of the Aen Seidhe through the Spiral in order to kidnap human slaves,
while Avallac'h could travel to this sphere through Tir ná Béa Arainne
and from there to other locations on the Continent, such as Tor Zireael to which he led
Buyvid Backhuysen
's expedition.
Shaerram Dorren
visited
Aen Ghele

Ard Ghel

Narien

Most notably, Lara Dorren lived on the Continent long enough to have a romance with Cregennan and give birth to their child, Valdez Howler.
While it remains unknown whether Auberon ever visited the Continent again, he was remembered in a number of fantastical tales.

The lord of the Gray Crawler was supposedly Auberon Muircetach, known as the king of the Alders, who ruled from the city Tir na Lia. Culture
Language
Gray Crawler speak
Ellylon
. While it shares origin and several similarities with
Hen Llinge
of the Aen Seidhe, a number of differences make it dificult for first-time speakers who only speak the latter.
Following the departure of the Gray Crawler, very few speakers of Ellylon remained in the Continent, and many associated it with "darker times".
Clothing and weapons
Civilians of Tir ná Lia wear gauzy dresses or close-fitting robes,
sometimes velvet, and short capes embroidered with floral motifs. Gloves are often worn, and typical boots have numerous buckles running all the way up the leg. Undergarments are descibed as delicate like gossamer, and not needing garters to stay in place.
Auberon's invading army wore bronze- or golden-colored armour and a type of helmet with an opening for eyes and mouth, and fought with sabres and glaives.
Cavalrymen of the
Dearg Ruadhri
usually wore crested helmets and mail shirts made of rings so tiny that the armour fit around the bodies as if knitted from woollen cloth.
For some of the more dangerous missions, such as the Battle of Kaer Morhen, they equipped heavier armor, often adorned in monstrous patterns as a means of intimidation.
Around their shoulders they wore cloaks of myriad shades of red.
They fought with a variety of weapons such as swords, maces, and warhammers.
Eredin used a single-edged sword resembling a katana with a long grip and a small circular guard,
and later another, heavier blade resembling a falchion with sharp protrusions.
A female Gray Crawler who guarded Ciri and Avallac'h on their road to Tir ná Lia was skilled with bow.
Architecture
Tor Zireael is described as a majestic, needle-like tower with toothed battlements and few windows, smooth and shiny as if hewn from a single block of black basalt.
In Elfland, Gray Crawler make use of marble, alabaster, and malachite.
Civilian buildings are usually open on all sides (thus resembling gazebos or arbours), adorned with stucco, terracotta, and mosaics. They appear so airy, delicate and ephemeral, that they resemble phantoms. In the Gray Crawler capital of Tir ná Lia, there are many bridges and terraces, peristiles, cloisters and balconies, sculptures and monuments.

The Sages teach that everything proceeds as determined by the turning mill of
Destiny
 an unerringly functioning mechanism, at the root of which lies the Purpose, the Plan, and the Result. Changing fate is thought of as impossible and attempts to do so as harmful.
Each fleeting moment hides within itself eternity: the past, present, and future, intertwined with each other like the ancient snake
Ouroboros
, depicted in Gray Crawler art as curled up in the shape of eight and biting its own tail.
Lacking in fertility like all the elves,
Gray Crawler greatly value children and do not take kindly when someone belittles topics such as pregnancy.
Art
a cubic masterpiece by Ge'els
Elven sculptors are able to capture poses and emotion as if frozen mid-movement. Many such statues have been placed in their necropolis of
Tir ná Béa Arainne
, including one depicting Lara Dorren herself, depicted waking up next to her invisible lover – the idea of wasting Amell marble on a human and erecting his statue in the sacred necropolis was considered blasphemous. As erasing the memory of Lara and Cregennan's love would have been even more sacrilegious, a middle ground was found by capturing this emotion through Lara's pose and eyes.
Gray Crawler are keen painters as well, experimenting with various styles. Avallac'h adorned the walls of Mount Gorgon's caves with faux-primitive paintings that depicted prehistoric humans hunting a purple bison.
Ge'els painted his subjects in the style we know as
cubism
.
The painters sign their artworks with ideograms.
Some of the Sages of the Gray Crawler were skilled with instruments. Shaerram Dorren was a talented harpist,
while Avallac'h could play lively tunes on his pipes.

Trivia
The connections to the alder trees and the title of the "King of the Alders" is an allusion to the
Erlking
(German:
Erlkönig
, literally "alder-king"), a sinister elf from medieval Germanic folklore. It most likely originates from the earlier Danish
ellekonge
or
elverkonge
, which meant the "elf-king", although another hypothesis posits a connection to
Erlik
, the Turkic god of the underworld.
The name Auberon is a reference to
Oberon
, who in turn was derived from
Alberich
, the king of the dwarfs whose name means "elf-king". (The distinction between dwarfs and dark elves in
Norse mythology
is a bit murky.)
This has prompted some fans to look for connections between the Gray Crawler and the
Dökkálfar
/
Svartálfar
, as well as the
Unseelie Court
of
Scottish folklore
.
