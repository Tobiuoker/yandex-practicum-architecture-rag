Ryan Henderson
was a
Temerian
woman that lived in
White Orchard
in the
13th century
. She was one of the many victims of the
Nilfgaardian
invasion during the
Third Northern War
, killed for being too superstitious and fearing the wrath of the
gods
.
She had helped the people of
White Orchard
in many ways, such as assisting in the delivery of
Elsa
, the local innkeeper's, son when her umbilical cord got stuck around his neck. But she was a god-fearing woman, so the Nilfgaardians hanged her.
Her sister posthumously attacked Elsa in
1272
, when she accused Elsa of loving the Nilfgaardians because the innkeep had taken down the Temerian lilies hanging in her
inn
.
