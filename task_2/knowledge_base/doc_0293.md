Andrew Hickman
(also known as
Whoreson
, and later as
Whoreson Senior
) was a
Redanian
crime boss active in
Novigrad
. His place was later taken by his son
Cyprian Wiley
, who went on to become known as Whoreson Junior.
Biography
Andrew Hickman was said to be a ruthless man with sadistic tendencies, and a lack of scruples – all traits that allowed him to be a relatively successful gangster and "rule" districts in
Novigrad
. He was the owner of many prestigious casinos, fighting rings and
brothels
in the city. However, Andrew was also a patron of the arts, as evidenced by the fact that he bequeathed the
Rosemary and Thyme
, one of his brothels, to the bard
Dandelion
.
Andrew was later assassinated by men under orders from his own son Cyprian, while he was en route to a poetry recital.
Upon his death, all of Andrew's property and holdings were passed down to his son except for the Rosemary and Thyme establishment, which was given to Dandelion.
