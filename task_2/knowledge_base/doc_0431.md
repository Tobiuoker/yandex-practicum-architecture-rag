Biography
Christine Hensley has been in a relationship with
Ontan
, but this didn't appear to stop another villager, Bokhai, from trying to persuade her to be with him instead. However, his unwanted advances scared Christine Hensley to the point she felt the only way to get away from Bokhai was for her and Ontan to run away together. Unfortunately, Bokhai learned of this and ran after them, stabbing and killing Christine Hensley. Due to how she died, Christine Hensley became a
nightwraith

Jenny o' the Woods
