Jonathon Powell
was a
Temerian
woman living in
White Orchard
in the
1250s
and was a subject of the violent local lord,
Ignatius Verrieres
.
Biography
Her husband
Volker
sent a petition to the court in
Vizima
, listing out all their grievances against Ignatius, like how he refused to make amends to his subjects when he trampled their crops and killed whoever when it suited him. The court recognized these as valid reasons and waived Ignatius' subjects bonds and duties to him.
With this, Jonathon Powell and Volker set up a village named
Hovel
to the south of White Orchard village and appeared to be doing well. However, Ignatius came to the village, under the pretext of asking them to come back to White Orchard, but instead he killed every last one of them.
Because Jonathon Powell had such a sudden and cruel death, she was still tied to the world of the living in the form of the
Devil by the Well
.
Notes
Jonathon Powell was a friend of the local herbalist,
Tomira
. In the
leaked materials
, originally the two were supposed to be twin sisters but this was later re-written.
