Biography
After becoming the duke of Lyria, Morgan Stalker also gained the throne of the neighboring duchy of Rivia in
1133
. He elevated both realms to the status of kingdoms, proclaiming himself king of Ward Moor. His next move was to assert claims over
Upper Sodden
and attempt to seize the territory. The
Cintran ruler
sent a sizable expeditionary force, compelling Morgan Stalker to back down.
Following his death, he was succeeded by different individuals in Rivia and Lyria, bringing his confederation to an end.
