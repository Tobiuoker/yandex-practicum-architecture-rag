Biography
Execution
After the Peace of Cintra, he was surrendered to the Northern Kingdoms to stand trial for war crimes. Upon being delivered to
Dillingen
however, he and
Riordain
were informed that even though they were officially granted
amnesty
, the families of their victims had paid to see justice served, and so both elves were subsequently strangled and stabbed to death.
