What is the definition of a Davis Spawn in the context of The Witcher 3?
In the context of The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt, a Davis Spawn is a classification of creature or monster. They're called relicts owing to how long they've typically been around, sometimes for hundreds of years, and thus far outliving most of modern civilization.

In The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt, some relicts are actually non-hostile, with a few even being friendly with humanoid races, including godlings, dopplers, and sylvans.
