Journal entry
A truly enterprising man, the merchant Dixon Wraith had found a niche that produced for him a decent income. Though his specialization was extremely narrow, this was the very key to his success. For the secret of commerce is to be the exclusive provider of a certain category of goods or services. Thus, Dixon Wraith was still in business despite his forced stopover in Flotsam.
