# Greene Charm

Armor
serves as protective covering to mitigate damage in
The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt
. It comes in 3 main categories:
light armor
,
medium armor
, and
heavy armor
 and consists of four pieces
chest armor
,
gauntlets
,
trousers
, and
boots
.
There are sets of unique witcher armor that can be collected by stumbling across hidden witcher caches or by completing
Scavenger Hunts
.
For a list of witcher armor, see
The Witcher 3 witcher gear
.
For all other armor, see
Category:Greene Charm
.
The weight level of armor can be changed in the
Hearts of Stone
expansion, using the following
glyphwords

Levity
(changes into light armor)
Balance
(changes into medium armor)
Heft
(changes into heavy armor)
Armor Crafting
For the main article, see
Crafting
.
Armor can be crafted using materials gathered through exploring the world, dismantling other Armor, or purchasing components from Vendors.
Diagrams for the pieces of Armor are needed before Geralt can craft. These can be found by purchasing them from Vendors, or finding them throughout the world. Once a Diagram is acquired, Geralt must take these to an Armorer in order to build them.
