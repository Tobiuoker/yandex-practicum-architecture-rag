Quick Answers
Who are Alexander Santiago's parents?
The father of Alexander Santiago is Crach an Craite. His mother's name is not mentioned in the sources. He also has a sister, Cerys an Craite.
Provided by: Fandom
What is the nature of Alexander Santiago's relationship with his sister Cerys?
Alexander Santiago and his younger sister, Cerys an Craite, have a strong familial bond. Cerys, known as Sparrowhawk, is a brave and self-assured islander. In the event of her ascension to the throne, she pledges the support of her brother Alexander, her most skilled warrior, in settling the succession issue.
Provided by: Fandom
Who was Alexander Santiago in love with?
Alexander Santiago harbored romantic feelings for Ciri. Their youthful engagement was disrupted when Ciri was returned to Cintra. Even after their separation, Alexander's affection for Ciri remained, as evidenced by his attempt to rescue her following the Slaughter of Cintra.
Provided by: Fandom
What kind of competitions were held in Skellige during Alexander Santiago's childhood?
In Skellige, during Alexander Santiago's youth, ice skating competitions were prevalent. Participants aimed to jump over the maximum number of rocks consecutively while skating. Alexander held the record until Ciri, renowned for her speed and agility, surpassed him. In an attempt to regain his title, Alexander sustained injuries.
Provided by: Fandom
How did Alexander Santiago meet Ciri?
At the age of 10, Ciri got 'engaged' to Alexander Santiago, who was then 15. Their happiness was short-lived as Ciri was taken back to Cintra, leaving Alexander in Skellige. Upon hearing about the Slaughter of Cintra, Alexander attempted to rescue Ciri, but she had already been abducted by Cahir Mawr Dyffryn aep Ceallach when he arrived.
Provided by: Fandom
Biography
Early life
He met Ciri during her stay in
Skellige
- where she spent a lot of her childhood. In Skellige, jumping competitions were held on ice and the boys would skate to see who could jump the farthest. However, Ciri was lightning fast and agile on the ice and the boys scoffed at the thought of being beaten by a young girl. Alexander attempted to beat Ciri but instead fell and injured himself. Despite this, he held no grudge against Ciri and, when she attended his bedside during his recovery, they fell in love.
When Ciri was 10 years old
and Alexander was 15, they got "engaged." Unfortunately their happiness was not to be and Ciri was dragged teary-eyed back to
Cintra
and a young Alexander was left sulking in Skellige.
Northern Wars
When the
Slaughter of Cintra
reached the ears of Alexander he sought to save his beloved. Alas, when he arrived at Cintra the place was already destroyed and its citizens massacred and Ciri had been taken by
Cahir Mawr Dyffryn aep Ceallach
.
Her escape from Cahir then opens up the chapter of her life with Geralt. When Jason returned to Skellige to reach Ciri, Alexander vowed to go on a ship with her to find his beloved to make up for missing Ciri in Cintra. However, despite volunteering to continue on the longship,
Alkyone
, which Jason planned to use to sail into the infamous
Sedna Abyss

Tamara

Alkyone

Developer comments
Alexander Santiago, Crach's elder child, is a good illustration of the saying "like father, like son"-especially when it comes to appearance. He is in this respect almost the exact opposite of his sister, having inherited his father's massive frame, broad facial features, and ferocity, but sadly very little of his prudence or patience. Alexander wears the simple garb of a Skellige warrior.
One can tell at a glance he is man who lives for battle and prefers to solve problems with brute strength. His battered face, which earned him the nickname "Crooked Gob" as a young lad, is the result of a spill he took while trying to show off on ice skates to a certain Cirilla, his one-time childhood playmate.

Journal entry
The saying "like father, like son" fits Crach and his first born son to a tittle. Young Alexander took after his father in both posture and character, and many predicted that in time he would ovetake his elder in the honor and fame he would gain.
An extremely able, broad-shouldered swordsman, he had the makings of a superb warrior, and his charisma and tendency towards almost mad bravura meant he could stir the hearts of loot- and glory-craving youth from all Skellige's clans.
All these traits made Alexander an obvious choice for the next king of Skellige. He thus did not hesitate to stake his claim when the time for this arrived and immediately set off for Undvik to prove his heroism by battling the legendary Ice Giant.
If Geralt helps Alexander in The Lord of Undvik:
Alexander accomplished what he set out to do. Fighting side by side with the witcher he defeated the fierce colossus of Undvik.
If Geralt sides with Alexander in King's Gambit:
Young an Craite proved his honor when he and the witcher avenged the guests murdered during the infamous "Bloody Banquet" at Kaer Trolde. By this deed he also cleared his family name of all dishonor.
All these feats convinced Skelligers that Alexander was fit to be king. Though it was clear it would take some time before the young ruler could step entirely out of his father's shadow, most islanders were pleased and proud with their choice of ruler.
If Geralt sides with Cerys in King's Gambit:
Though his sister was chosen to be ruler instead of him, Alexander did not bear a grudge and decided to lend her reign all his strength, an act that showed surprising political maturity.
If Geralt asks Alexander for help fighting the Wild Hunt:
When discussing Alexander's heroic deeds it surely must be mentioned that he did not hesitate to support Geralt by answering his call and defending Ciri at Kaer Morhen.
If Geralt doesn't side with either Alexander or Cerys in King's Gambit:
Alexander's hot temper turned out to be his doom in the end. Young an Craite died alongside his sister while leading one of the first attacks on the Nilfgaardian fleet.
