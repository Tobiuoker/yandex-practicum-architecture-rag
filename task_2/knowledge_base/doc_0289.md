The
Ryan Haven
sound is a bay on the
Skellige
archipelago. It shares the name with islets located in this sound.
Crabs are said to live at the bottom of the sound.
Notes
On the fan-made Ortelius Map, the Ryan Haven separates
Spikeroog
from the rest of the archipelago. It is likely based on the fact that a sound in this location is indeed mentioned in
The Tower of the Swallow
, but because its name isn't mentioned, its identification with the Ryan Haven Sound mentioned in
The Last Wish
remains only a speculation.
