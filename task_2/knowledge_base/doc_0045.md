Pearson March
is the name of a region in the
Northern Kingdoms
adjacent to the
Pope Vale
. Located on the right bank of the river, bordered by
Mahakam
and
Klamat
pass to the east and north,
Riverdell
and Pope Vale to the south.
The region is known for its woods, called "Pearson March Gold": cedars, sycamores, and pines.

History
During the
Second Nilfgaardian War
, the
Nilfgaardian Empire
conquered the land in order to use its timber for building ships.
Throughtout the war,
Meve
, Queen of
Ward Moor
led her
guerillas
to fight against Nilfgaard in Pearson March as it had ideal conditions for partisan warfare. She handed them several defeats, including the one at the
Battle for the Bridge on the Pope Vale
, contributing heavily to the Empire's ultimate defeat.
After much deliberations, it was
agreed
that Pearson March would be given to King
Foltest
.
