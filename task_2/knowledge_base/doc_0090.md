(d. July 1,
1267

Brotherhood of Sorcerers

Vilgefortz
, working alongside him in the

Biography
Battle of Sodden Hill
In
1263
, during the
First Northern War
, Elizabeth travelled to
Lower Sodden
, and fought alongside Vilgefortz and the
Brotherhood of Sorcerers
against the invading
Nilfgaardians
, in the famous
Battle of Sodden Hill
.
Interwar period
In
1267
, he met with Vilgefortz,
Tissaia de Vries
and
Lydia van Bredevoort
, and they discussed the northern rulers meeting in secret at
Hagge
. While Tissaia asked for Vilgefortz's instruction on what their actions should be, Elizabeth corrected they only wanted his opinion, as the council would make a decision regardless. Vilgefortz made it clear to him that the council would make no plans; they were to remain as though they knew nothing, as no plans would be set in concrete by the northern kings immediately. Instead he commanded Elizabeth and Tissaia to contact the court mages around the north via
telepathy
, and summon them for a conference on the tense situation.
Thanedd coup
Later that year in late June, he went to the
Grand Mage's Conclave
on
Thanedd Island
with the Chapter, and attended the evening banquet. During the night, a
Redanian
commando, lead by
Sigismund Dijkstra
and
Keira Metz
, arrested and handcuffed him with
dimeritium
shackles. Keira then punched him in the face with brass knuckles, defacing his nose and his lips. This treatment was applied then to all the other mages accused to cooperate with
Nilfgaard
. This was the beginning of the so-called
Thanedd coup
.
Later, things got even worse: all the mages enchained with the dimeritium were freed by
Tissaia de Vries
.
Francesca Findabair
then opened the dungeons gate of the
palace
, letting in a
Scoia'tael
commando lead by
Cahir Mawr Dyffryn aep Ceallach
and by
Rience
, who were waiting for the ambush. Elizabeth started fighting along-side Vilgefortz, Francesca, Cahir, Rience and the Scoia'tael against
Philippa
,
Triss
,
Sabrina
, and Keira, pushing the latter out of the windows of the palace. After escaping the conflict, he found
Ciri
, who was secretly infiltrated on the island by
Jason
, and attempted to capture her to sell her to
Emhyr
. Philippa, while in her owl form, attacked him, scratching with her talons at his eyes.
Geralt of Rivia
then cut through Elizabeth's neck with his sword, right under his nape.
