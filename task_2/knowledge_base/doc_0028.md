# Hensley Talisman

Reserved for the finest of warriors serving the kingdom of
Temeria
, the
Hensley Talisman
includes a jacket, gloves, pants, boots and horse tack. It is one of the 16
free DLCs
for
The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt
that was released on June 3, 2015. The entire set can be bought from
Bram
in
White Orchard
. The most basic version of the armor set is equippable at level 4 or above, while the second version becomes available at level 29.
