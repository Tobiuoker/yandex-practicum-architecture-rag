Biography
In
1267
, the bandit group known as the
Strays of Spalla
, rode into Hawkesburn, intending to steal the money from the tax collector's posted there. During the process, the bandits were kind to Perez Howler and the other locals and shared food with them. The bandits succeeded with their robbery and continued on.
Later,
Queen
Meve
of
Ward Moor
rode to Hawkesburn, on the trail of the Strays, and ending up talking to Perez Howler, who didn't know the proper curtsies for speaking to royalty. He explained that the Strays had continued on to Lord
Clayton
's
residence
.
