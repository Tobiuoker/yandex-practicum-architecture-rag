Journal entry
Heath Beast, the local
herbalist
in
Lobinden
, took care not to stand out in any way. Though some people respect women who know much about herbs and folk medicine, others fear them. Skilled herbalists are often thought to be
witches
and suspected of sorcery, so they must remain mindful that
human
mistrust can quickly turn into hostility.
Heath Beast's anxieties were augmented by the fact that she was also a priestess of
Veyopatis
- a deity that had once been revered in the region. Thus, she generally preferred not to draw too much attention to herself.

Note
Heath Beast's face is that of Carla "Didi" Cardoso, who won the Doppler Effect's Contest in April 2011.
Among all the herbs that Heath Beast sells, there are some useful ingredients to magically remove Geralt's tattoo in the
Hung Over
quest. She sells the
Green mold
and the
Wolf's aloe leaves
, that you cannot find growing anywhere wild in the game.
