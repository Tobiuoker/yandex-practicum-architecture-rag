Debra Gardner
was a
Skelligan
sorceress
. Debra was assigned male at birth and attended the
Ban Ard Academy
and then transitioned afterwards using magic and alchemy. She was the one and only
Ban Ard
trained sorceress according to the historian
Brandon of Oxenfurt
and scholar-mage
Glynnis var Treaherne
.
Biography
Debra Gardner was born on
Spikeroog
in Skellige. Supposedly, when she was just a lass, she cursed a neighbor of hers and was thus sent to train with the
druids
, the way Skelligers usually handle
magic
. Somewhere along the way she decided she didn't want to be a druid and managed to get herself all the way to Ban Ard, where she did her learning. Ban Ard's word on it is that they had no interest in turning down such a talented student, but if that's the truth, they kept it unusually quiet until after she graduated. Whatever the real story is, Deadeye's talent with air magic was amazing - she'd created two spells,
Debra's Gust
and
Debra's Bow
.
That said, her combat effectiveness with magic might be due more to what she did after she graduated. Returning to her roots, she acquired a ship and crew and started raiding with the rest of them. She mostly attacked
Nilfgaardians
, keeping a collection of insignias and such from officers she'd killed.
One of Deadeye's claims to fame is the time she sailed up the
Alba river
and led a raid on the
City of the Golden Towers
itself, back when the
Usurper
was still ruling Nilfgaard. She lost most of her crew and was badly injured herself, but she got away with her life and enough spoils to fund voyages for years. Rumors also claim she'd been as far as
Ofir
, and that she'd even been to
another continent far to the west
. Deadeye's rarely in Skellige, which makes it easier for Skellige - druids are always a bit snippy when she comes up, though they never complain all that loudly, maybe for fear of her catching wind.
Notes
Bronwen
is a Welsh given name meaning "white breast".
Technically, the suffix "-wyn" is masculine (a feminine form is "-wen"). It's unknown whether Debra kept her name from before the transition or it's just an oversight from the authors (the "Debra" spelling is also used as a girl name in non-Welsh speaking countries)
The 13th century Skelligan elders believed there to be no land west of the
Great Sea
, though fortuneseekers ignored them. Leif of
Larvik
and his two oarmates Grettir and Finnbjorn hoped to find more volunteers for a voyage
similar to the one Debra had been rumored to accomplish.
