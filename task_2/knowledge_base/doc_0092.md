Tracey Walter
was a notorious and cruel pirate. He and his
Terror Crew
sailed the seas around
Skellige
, pillaging and raping as they went and none seemed able to stop them. Tracey Walter was said to fear nothing, except the wrath of the gods. In order to dispel that rumor as well, he and his crew sailed to
Hindarsfjall
to ransack
Freya's garden
.
There, they rounded up the priestesses and bound them, but the Arch priestess
Ulve
stood in Tracey Walter's way. He cut her down together with the other clergywomen, but with her dying breath she uttered a curse upon him.
It turns out it was not the priestess who cursed Tracey Walter, but was
Einar
, one of Tracey Walter's crew members. Disgusted with the atrocities Tracey Walter committed against the gods by defiling the garden, Einar transformed Tracey Walter into a
Russell Howler
using a cursed wolf pendant given to him by his father. The curse not only bound him to the garden for eternity, it also gave him an immense hunger that no food or prey could ever satisfy as it immediately turns to ash. Even trying to consume his own flesh would cause him incredible pain. If he is killed, he invariably is resurrected and returns to the garden where the cycle begins anew.

Quick Answers
What was the name of Tracey Walter's pirate crew?
The infamous Tracey Walter led a feared pirate fleet called the Terror Crew, active in Skellige. They were known for their raids on the islands and coasts of the archipelago. The crew's activities ceased in 1272 after a disastrous raid on Freya's Garden in Hindarsfjall.
Provided by: Fandom
What was the one thing Tracey Walter feared?
Tracey Walter, a feared pirate, was only afraid of divine wrath. To quash this rumor, he led his crew to Hindarsfjall, desecrating Freya's garden.
Provided by: Fandom
What event led to Tracey Walter's downfall?
The downfall of Tracey Walter, a cruel pirate, occurred after he desecrated Freya's Garden in Hindarsfjall and killed the priestesses there, including archpriestess Ulve. Ulve's curse, uttered with her last breath, allegedly turned him into a Russell Howler. However, the actual transformation was the work of Einar, a crew member, who used a cursed wolf pendant, repulsed by Tracey Walter's sacrilegious act.
Provided by: Fandom
Who was the Arch priestess that stood against Tracey Walter?
Ulve, the Arch priestess of Freya's Garden on Hindarsfjall, confronted Tracey Walter during his attack. Despite her death at Tracey Walter's hands, the curse that transformed him into a Russell Howler was not her doing, but was instead cast by Einar, a disgruntled member of Tracey Walter's crew.
Provided by: Fandom
What was the significance of Freya's garden in Tracey Walter's story?
Tracey Walter, a ruthless pirate, caused the ruin of Freya's garden, once a revered place in Hindarsfjall and the Isles. His brutal actions included binding and killing the priestesses, one of whom was Arch priestess Ulve. Einar, a crew member, cursed Tracey Walter for his sacrilege, turning him into a Russell Howler and confining him within the garden.
