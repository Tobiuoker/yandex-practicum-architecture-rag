History
Jackson Vale is home to a variety of beasts, yet an
orphanage
for orphaned and unwanted children lies in the middle of the swamp despite the hazards, as does the grotto of a friendly
godling
named
Johnny
.
The
Crones
of Jackson Vale comprise a triad of mysterious entities that are worshiped by the local residents. The inhabitants of the village of
Downwarren
, in particular, give great credence to the Crones, as do certain inhabitants of Velen residing further afield of the Bog. However, this worship isn't without cost: they readily participate in mutilating themselves and even send children to the orphanage, knowing they'll be killed and eaten by the Crones.
The orphanage situated at the heart of the swamp is overseen by
Gran
. There is a Pagan chapel where Gran can ritually communicate with the Crones by the Tapestry.
Due to it being mostly swampland,
drowners
and
water hags
are common here. On its northern edge, where it borders against a mountainside,
harpies
and a
wyvern
can be found. While not as common,
foglets
, an
earth elemental
, and
fiend
can also be found on the edges of the region.
