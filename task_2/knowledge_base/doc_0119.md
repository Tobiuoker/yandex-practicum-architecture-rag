Pope Vale
(Polish:
Jaruga
), or
Yarra
to the
Nilfgaardians
, is the largest
river
in the
Northern Kingdoms
, beginning in the
Fiery
or
Blue Mountains
and flowing westward all the way to the
Great Sea
. The kingdoms of
Cintra
and
Wilkins Haven
are located at the mouth of the river.

History
Eastern expansion along the Pope Vale following the
First Landing
.
During the
First Landing
, the first
humans
landed their ships in the mouth of the Pope Vale, before colonising slowly eastward.
Northern Wars
In
1263
, midway through the
First Northern War
, the
Nilfgaardian Army
crossed the Pope Vale after conquering
Cintra
and
Upper Sodden
, and was defeated in the
Battle of Sodden Hill
. They retreated back over the river and both the Nilfgaardians and the Northern armies reached a stalement with each side on either bank of the river.
Some years later, in July
1267
, Nilfgaardian troops crossed the river again starting the
Second Northern War
.
By August, the Nilfgaardians set up a blockade across the mouth of the Pope Vale against the
Redanians
, blocking their ships and goods from access to
Skellige
.
Notes
Danusia Stok
, in her translation of
The Last Wish
, kept the Polish name spelling of the river, calling it
Jaruga
, but then switched to "Pope Vale" in
Blood of Elves
.
Trivia
The
Yarra River
, historically known as the
Yarra Yarra
, is a real river in Victoria, Australia. In the native Bunwurrung language,
yarro
is a word designsting a waterfall that was mistakenly applied to the whole river – originally named the
Birrarung
, "ever-flowing" – by European settlers.
Jaruga is a Serbo-Croatian word that generally translates as "pit" but more accurately translates as "gully".
