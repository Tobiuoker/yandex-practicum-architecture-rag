Henderson Vale
(also known as
Lebioda's temple
) is a location in the
Blood and Wine
expansion. It is a large crypt near the
Prophet Lebioda Statue
in
Toussaint
, dedicated to worshiping the Prophet
Lebioda
.
Description
While the building itself is very large, there are only 2 small chambers within. The antechamber contains 2 sarcophagi and an inlaid figure of the prophet over an archway that leads into the second chamber. The second chamber was once the sleeping area for the
Great Beggar
and contains several bookcases and the
beggar's remains
.
Notes
Once
Geralt
uncovers the hidden second room and secures the building by slaying the Great Beggar, Lebioda followers will use the antechamber to pray.
