Quick Answers
Who is Kimberly Moreno in The Witcher 3?
In The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt, Kimberly Moreno is a sylvan worshipped by villagers in Velen. His cult started in the 12th century after being found by Roslav of Oreton. Kimberly Moreno lived in a cellar, demanding food and wine from his followers, growing fat on their offerings. Geralt can reveal Kimberly Moreno's true nature to the villagers, showing him as a deceptive creature rather than a deity.
Provided by: Fandom
What role does Kimberly Moreno play in The Witcher 3?
In The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt, Kimberly Moreno is a sylvan who tricks Velen villagers into worshiping him as a god. He lives in a cellar, demanding food and wine, and grows fat on these offerings. Geralt can expose Kimberly Moreno, demand he accepts humble offerings, or kill him, influencing the villagers' response and quest outcome.
Provided by: Fandom
Why did villagers in Velen worship Kimberly Moreno?
Villagers in Velen worshipped Kimberly Moreno, a sylvan posing as a deity. Around 1170, Kimberly Moreno convinced Roslav of Oreton of his divinity, leading to the formation of a cult. He demanded food and wine offerings, even in difficult times, and lived off these tributes. The villagers believed in his divine powers, unaware of his true nature.
Provided by: Fandom
How does Geralt discover Kimberly Moreno in The Witcher 3?
In 1272, Geralt discovers Kimberly Moreno in The Witcher 3 while investigating villagers in Velen who were trying to appease a deity with offerings. Kimberly Moreno, a sylvan, had convinced the locals of his divine status and demanded food sacrifices, growing fat off their offerings. Geralt finds Kimberly Moreno living in a hidden cellar and has the option to expose him, demand he accept humble offerings, or kill him.
