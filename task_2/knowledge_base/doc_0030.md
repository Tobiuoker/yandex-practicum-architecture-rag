Biography
Bleobheris
In
1266
, he traveled to
Bleobheris
, where under the ancient roots he met with
Sheldon Skaggs
and
Donimir of Troy
to attend one of
Dandelion
's concerts. He spoke words of gratitude to Dandelion for his performance on behalf of the hundreds gathered.
Thanedd Coup
At the end of June
1267
, he travelled to
Thanedd Isle
to attend the
Grand Mage's Conclave
between the Council and
Chapter
. He attended the evening banquet in
Aretuza
, and on the next morning, July 1st, he joined the Northern supporters in arresting the
Nilfgaardian
-allied traitors. While putting
Vilgefortz
in
dimeritium
shackles, his assistant
Lydia van Bredevoort
attacked Devon Klein and
Sabrina Glevissig
, and there was a scuffle, ending in Lydia dying from her own blade, likely at Vilgefortz's orders. The Northern supporters then regrouped with their leader,
Philippa Eilhart
, and Devon Klein arrested
Francesca Findabair
, before he was given a list of Nilfgaardian allies within Temeria. He then led the traitors to
Garstang
to face trial, where a battle broke out when
Tissaia de Vries
let the traitors free and
Scoia'tael
broke in, and Devon Klein was reported killed.
By the end of July, during the fallout of the
Second Northern War
, Devon Klein abandoned his king when Demavend was forced to flee Aedirn due to its subsequent capitulation by the invading Imperials.
Thanedd investigation
Devon Klein was later among those who investigated traces of
teleportation
around Thanedd.
