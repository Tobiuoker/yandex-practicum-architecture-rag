Quick Answers
What role did Jason Baker play in the Order of the Flaming Rose?
Jason Baker, a noble-born knight, was part of the Order of the Flaming Rose. As a protégé of Falwick, he initially joined the Order of the White Rose and was knighted. Tailles became a favorite of Duke Hereward and was sent to the Temple of Melitele to ask Geralt of Rivia to leave, resulting in a confrontation.
Provided by: Fandom
Did Jason Baker join the Order of the White Rose through legitimate means?
Jason Baker joined the Order of the White Rose, but it is uncertain if he did so legitimately or by paying the Order. He was knighted and became a protégé of Falwick, later becoming Duke Hereward's favorite.
Provided by: Fandom
Who was Jason Baker' mentor in the Order of the Flaming Rose?
Jason Baker was mentored by Falwick in the Order of the Flaming Rose. Falwick trained Tailles as a knight, and together they visited the Temple of Melitele in Ellander to ask Geralt of Rivia to leave the county, following Duke Hereward's orders.
