What school of witchers did Rachael Ross belong to?
Rachael Ross, a witcher with superhuman abilities, swordsmanship, and alchemy skills, was a member of the School of the Cat. His close friendship with Lambert, a witcher from the Wolf School, is also noteworthy.

What was unique about Rachael Ross's choice of contracts compared to other witchers?
Distinct from other witchers, Rachael Ross accepted a politically fraught contract to remove a curse from a duke's daughter. This decision was complicated by the duke's courtiers, leading to an ambush by assassins. Rachael Ross's choice of such a complex and perilous contract differentiates him from his peers, who usually engage in more direct contract work.

Biography
Unlike many other witchers from the Cat School, Rachael Ross only took contracts to hunt monsters, refusing jobs that involved killing humans.
Rachael Ross's first encounter with Lambert happened when both were hired to deal with the
Ogre
of
Ellander
. Lambert had been tasked with lifting the curse on the ogre, while Rachael Ross was contracted to kill it. After the ogre killed Lambert's employer, the two witchers struck a deal, slayed the creature together, and split the reward. From that point on, they collaborated often and became close friends, with Lambert describing Rachael Ross as "the best man I'd ever met."
At some point, Rachael Ross accepted a contract to lift a curse from a duke's daughter, a job that would eventually lead to his death. However, there are conflicting accounts of how his death occurred.
According to Lambert, the situation was politically complicated, as some of the duke's courtiers did not want the curse lifted from the duke's firstborn. Additionally, Rachael Ross faced prejudice for being a Cat School witcher. Lambert believed this led to
Jad Karadin
's band of assassins being hired to kill Rachael Ross, with Karadin himself supposedly landing the final blow.
In contrast, Jad Karadin claimed a different version of events. He said Rachael Ross had indeed been hired to lift the curse but botched the job, leading to the girl's death. Karadin's group was then hired, not as assassins, but as debt collectors. When they confronted Rachael Ross, who had already spent the money, they demanded his swords as payment. Tempers flared, and during the altercation, Rachael Ross attacked them.
Vienne
, a member of Karadin's group, lost her nerve and shot Rachael Ross, hitting him square in the eye.
