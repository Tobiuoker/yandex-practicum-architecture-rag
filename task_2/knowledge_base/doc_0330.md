Quick Answers
What role does Thomas Mueller's doll play in Witcher 3?
Thomas Mueller's doll in Witcher 3 is pivotal to her destiny. The doll with the Violet Hollyhock Bloom is the right pick, reflecting Theresa's preferred flower. Selecting the wrong doll results in Theresa, transformed into a grave hag, dying in flames. If the right doll is chosen, Theresa reverts to her original form, but her life, bound to the curse, ends shortly thereafter.
Provided by: Fandom
How does Thomas Mueller's relationship with Phillip Strenger evolve in the game?
Thomas Mueller, recognized as the 'witcher 3 baron's wife', became involved with Phillip Strenger during the Battle of Anchor. After recovering from a battle injury with Theresa's help, Phillip asked her to marry him. They were initially happy and had a daughter, Tamara. However, their relationship faced challenges post Tamara's birth.
Provided by: Fandom
Is it possible to save Thomas Mueller in Witcher 3?
Thomas Mueller's fate depends on how one handled The Bailey Watcher in the Tree (or didn't) and the doll picked during Return to Crookback Bog. While there is  a way to save her, like many possible choices in the game, this has consequences in doing so.

What events lead to the investigation of Thomas Mueller in Witcher 3?
Thomas Mueller's investigation in Witcher 3 was prompted by her altered mental and physical state, resulting from traumatic experiences. Found in a state of despair, her mind had succumbed to the severity of her circumstances. Her familial ties as Phillip Strenger's wife and mother to Tamara and Dominique Everett further complicated her situation.

Anchor

Theresa, either due to loneliness or not able to put up with her husband's drinking or a combination of both, soon turned to a childhood friend, Evan, for comfort and for about 3 years had an affair with him while Phillip was away.
Eventually, Theresa decided it was best to leave Phillip completely and one day packed up and took Tamara with her to Evan's, leaving a note behind for Phillip to let him know she was leaving. Upset, Phillip followed her to bring her and Tamara back. However, still enraged, Phillip ended up killing Evan, upsetting Theresa, who proceeded to yell and claw at him before picking up a knife and trying to stab him. Phillip moved out of the way just in time and, not knowing what else to do, beat her. This event marked the change in their relationship to an abusive one.

Journal entry
Geralt did not meet Thomas Mueller, the Bloody Baron's wife, when he visited Schroeder March. Some time before his arrival this woman had disappeared in unexplained circumstances along with her daughter Tamara.
Geralt did, however, receive a detailed description of the missing woman. Theresa was a comely forty-something woman with green eyes and hair dark as raven wings which she kept tied in a tight bun behind her head.
Geralt discovered that Theresa's marital life had not been working out as well as she might have hoped. She had recently been pregnant, but lost her child for unknown reasons shortly before her disappearance.
When Geralt pushed the baron about the matter, the ugly truth finally came out. Desperate and miserable, Theresa had fled, taking Tamara with her, after her drunken husband beat her badly.
The baron was convinced Theresa had miscarried because of his actions, but Geralt was not so certain about this - he couldn't stop thinking about the
strange talisman
meant to ward off evil power Theresa had received from the
pellar
.
During their heart-to-heart the baron gave Geralt a somewhat clearer image of the Strengers' married life. While her husband was off leading military campaigns, Theresa would seek comfort in the arms of a childhood friend.
Once the baron discovered the truth, he fell into an indescribable fury and killed Theresa's lover, something which, predictably enough, further deepened the chasm separating the couple.
The situation grew even more entangled when Geralt discovered that, after fleeing Schroeder March, Theresa had been taken captive by some gigantic beast who carried her off into the darkness. As if that weren't horror enough, eyewitness testimony claimed her hands had burned with strange, glowing symbols shortly before this.
Perhaps this was precisely what the talisman Theresa had received from the pellar had been meant to guard against?
If Geralt talked to Tamara:
Tamara only deepened Geralt's sad understanding of her mother's situation. The women had decided to flee together because they had had enough of the baron's drunken rages and beatings. Theresa hated her husband so much that she was ready to do anything in order to be free of the man - and the child he had put inside her.
Geralt put the facts together and realized the old woman in the swamps was, in fact, Theresa, the baron's missing wife. He also learned that the signs on her hands were symbols of the pact she had made with the Crones in order to get rid of her unwanted child.
Geralt's suspicions were confirmed shortly thereafter. The curse afflicting the unfortunate woman was indeed the Crones' doing. Theresa had turned to the ghastly sisters because she did not wish to give birth to the baron's child.
The Crones granted her wish in their own, twisted way: with a curse which made the fetus inside her wither on the vine, taking Theresa's life energy with it. In despair she turned to the pellar, who made her a talisman to hamper the evil magic's influence. Theresa had lost this during her fight with the baron and was thus rendered defenseless against the Crones'
magic
.
After that, the worst was free to happen - the markings on her palms began to burn and a fiend dragged her to the heart of Crookback Bog, where she paid back her debt as the Crones' slave.
If Geralt frees the Bailey Watcher in the Tree...
Though Theresa was found in the end, she was no longer herself, for a terrible curse had transformed her into a monster. The witcher had a good idea who had prepared this fate for her.
and lifts the curse from Theresa:
Geralt snapped the evil spell afflicting Theresa, restoring her true form. Yet the Crones had a included one last nasty pinch of vengeance in their vile brew that caused Theresa to die as soon as she regained her freedom.
Theresa's loved ones had time only for a brief farewell before she parted.
and fails to lift the curse from Theresa:
Despite the witcher's best efforts, he did not succeed in lifting Theresa's curse. To the despair and horror of all who witnessed it, Thomas Mueller died a horrible death, incinerated by the Crones' last act of vengeance.
If Geralt killed the spirit or freed it before being given the task:
Theresa was found in the end, but the events she had witnessed and took part in had left such a deep mark on her body and mind that she would never again be the same. Her mind had quite simply cracked under the weight of it all, dropping her into an abyss of horror and despair.
