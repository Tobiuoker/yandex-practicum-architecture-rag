Thomas Hold
, also known as
Holloway
is a mostly-walled village in
Velen
, located just outside of
Oxenfurt
, that was under the protection of the
Redanian
army. While villagers here mined stone, the army also brought in
Nilfgaardian
prisoners of war to help with production.
As Redanians later
moved out of the village, exploitation of the inhabitants labor ceased, but they were preyed on by local bandits.
Map description
Over twenty years ago a certain Bartolomeo, known as "Badger" on account of certain characteristics aspects of his coiffure, discovered a rich deposit of high-quality stone on this spot. He bought the land for a song, then leased it back to local peasants before heading off to
Kovir
, where he lives the life of a rich and powerful townsman to this day.
