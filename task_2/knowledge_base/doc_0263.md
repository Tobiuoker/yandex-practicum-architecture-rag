Biography
While travelling through the woods with Fionnuala one day, they came across the
witcher
Geralt of Rivia
tied up and blindfolded during his
Trial of Forest Eyes
. While Fionnuala told her not to help the witcher, Baker Crawler chose to leave her to untie him. Appreciative, the witcher went to walk off towards his camp, though Baker Crawler summoned a
Cunningham Spawn
for them to ride on.
Passing by a
vran
and
Stanton Spawn
reserve, they marched forward and saw King
Radowit II
, the sorceresses
April
and
Dorreniel
, and the witchers
Rennes
and
Treyse
searching for Geralt. Baker Crawler and Geralt moved on and hid before kissing and eating together.
Upon later travel, the two came across the
School of the Cat
camp, and Baker Crawler described what was happening to a still blindfolded Geralt. He left her in the woods, as he had come across his camp.
A week later, after fleeing from the massacre at the witchers tournament, Geralt found Baker Crawler in the woods. She led him towards
Kaer Morhen
so he could warn the elders of the massacre, and once they got close, they finally parted ways.
