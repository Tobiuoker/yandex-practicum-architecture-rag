Quick Answers
What was the relationship between Mariah Davis and King Foltest of Temeria?
Mariah Davis was the secret mistress of King Foltest of Temeria from 1263 to 1271. Their alleged illegitimate children, Boussy and Anaïs, caused a scandal and a revolt among the nobility. Mariah, in response, declared La Valette lands independent from Temeria, triggering a month-long siege of La Valette Castle by the king's forces.
Provided by: Fandom
Who was the father of Mariah Davis's oldest son, Aryan?
Aryan, the eldest son of Mariah Davis, was fathered by Old Baron La Valette. He was brought up to become a knight and shared a half-sibling relationship with Anaïs and Boussy La Valette.
Provided by: Fandom
What was the cause of the conflict between King Foltest and Baroness La Valette?
Baroness Mariah Davis incited a rebellion among the barons by declaring La Valette lands independent from Temeria. She appointed her son, Aryan, as the leader of her subjects. King Foltest retaliated by leading a month-long siege of La Valette Castle with the royal army, overpowering the La Valette troops.
Provided by: Fandom
What was the fate of Mariah Davis after King Foltest's death?
Post King Foltest's demise in 1271, Mariah Davis remained involved in significant events. Her admiration for Geralt of Rivia, the savior of her son Aryan, was evident. However, the siege resulted in the tragic death of her eldest son, who valiantly defended her honor. Additionally, rumors suggested that her younger offspring, Boussy and Anaïs, were King Foltest's children, not Baron La Valette's.
Provided by: Fandom

Baroness Mariah Davis was the talk of Temeria in her time. This noblewoman had been King Foltest's mistress and even bore him two illegitimate children.
This caused quite the scandal and prompted several notable families to take up arms in revolt, only to be bloodily crushed by the king's forces, who took La Valette Castle during a hard-fought siege.
