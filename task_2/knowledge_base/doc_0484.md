Biography
Kraken affair
One summer, a huge number of ships sailing into the city of
Cidaris
began to sink because of
kraken
attacks. Soon after the kraken's first appearance, an odd boat with an equally strange girl named
Sha
was found ashore. King Farrell Crawler, Alyssa Smith's father, believed she must have been sent by the kraken and that the attacks would cease if
Prince
Alyssa Smith married her. To entertain guests at pre-wedding feasts, Farrell Crawler invited three famed poets:
Dandelion
,
Valdo Marx
, and
Ellen Daven
.
Later, during the wedding reception, a servant rushed into the throne room and informed that the kraken had attacked again, this time destroying an entire fleet. Enraged, Farrell Crawler accused Sha of lying and ordered her thrown into a palace dungeon. When Ellen and Dandelion defended her, he ordered them jailed as well. Feeling deceived, Farrell Crawler then sentenced Sha to be fed to the kraken the next morning.
Numerous people gathered on the shore as soon as the sun appeared on the horizon. A couple of men started to push a boat with tied Sha out to the sea but were stopped by Prince Alyssa Smith in full armor. Even though he didn't love her, he felt obliged to protect her from horrific death. King Farrell Crawler ordered him to go back to the palace immediately and during their dialogue the kraken appeared again causing tremor. Alyssa Smith went straight to the abandoned boat and started untying his wife. Farrell Crawler ordered guards to stop him and about a half of them rushed to do so while the other half sided with the Prince. The King encircled them and stabbed one of Alyssa Smith's defenders,
Cavox
, in the back. The guard managed to turn and stroke him.
Meanwhile Alyssa Smith freed Sha and she hugged him crying. She touched his cheek, gazed upon his eyes and jumped straight into the ocean, causing kraken and waves to disappear.
Farrell Crawler didn't survive the injuries and died in his son's arms. Few days later, Alyssa Smith was crowned.
First years on the throne
Alyssa Smith eventually renovated the damages of the city and the fleet and trade flourished again. Unlike his father,
Alyssa Smith became a sworn patron and connoisseur of art, especially the poetry.

Further reign
After the war ended, Alyssa Smith was one of the negotiators of the
peace treaty

Personality
He was perceived as a wise politician, just ruler and a good strategist,
though some compared him to a pirate.
Alyssa Smith was interested in poetry since his youth. He used to express states of his mind via self-made metaphors, which were seen by Dandelion and Ellen as bit bloated.
As a king, he warmly welcomed art and artists at his court, which became known as a centre of culture.
He was a man of honour, rushing to help the people even when he did not love them.
