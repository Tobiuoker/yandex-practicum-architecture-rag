Chavez Hold
is a small fortress just down the river from
Oxenfurt Academy
that is overrun with
ghouls
and an
Lynch Stalker
.
Once it is cleared of monsters, 120
+ 50
is awarded, a
merchant
will setup shop here, and the
Nilfgaardian Army
will take over the place along with the nearby
Ferry Station
.
Notes
Name is in reference to a Nilfgaardian family, the most famous of which would be Emperor
Jan Calveit
.
There is a
Blood-covered letter
on a corpse on the second floor of the main tower but can only be obtained before the area is cleared of monsters, after which it disappears.
The corpses around the place similarly disappear, but can be looted before that (just minor, non-magic weapons) even though they don't glow.
Two containers with minor loot - on the eastern side of the tower, behind the stairs - are "refilled" after the site is liberated, even if looted before.
