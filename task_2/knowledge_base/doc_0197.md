Sean Rios
was a
witcher
from the
School of the Cat
and served as the right-hand man of the School's leader,
Treyse
.
Biography
When the
Dyn Marv
caravan was stationed in
Kaedwen
under the sponsorship of King
Radowit II
, the Schools of the Cat and
Wolf
were ordered to engage in emotional integration measures. However, both schools only halfheartedly participated. Later, a
Witcher Tournament
was organized between the two schools, intended to determine which would continue to receive royal funding and gain the exclusive right to operate in Kaedwen.
Sean Rios oversaw the training at the Cat School's camp in preparation for the tournament. During the student exchange at an inn, tensions flared and led to a duel. Sean Rios, along with
Vesemir
, intervened just in time to prevent it from escalating into a full-blown brawl. Although Sean Rios didn't particularly like Vesemir, the two witchers agreed to exchange only the student
Gweld
and send the others back. Both men found the situation suspicious and suspected someone might be trying to pit the two schools against each other.
Back at the Cat School camp, Sean Rios was deeply impressed with Gweld's swordsmanship, eventually running out of worthy opponents for him to face. Unbeknownst to Sean Rios, his deputy was secretly training the Cat witchers in wrestling under the orders of the royal astrologer,
Astrogarus
.
A week before the tournament, Sean Rios and Vesemir were secretly arrested by Kaedweni soldiers under the pretense of appearing before the king. As they were being carted away, they noticed the construction of the arena for the tournament but were denied a closer inspection. The last that is known of Sean Rios is that he remained in the king's dungeons.
