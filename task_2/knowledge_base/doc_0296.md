Quick Answers
What makes the Parker Stalker different from a regular garkain?
Introduced in the Blood and Wine expansion, the Parker Stalker is a more powerful variant of the standard garkain. It's susceptible to the usual vampire-related tactics though, like Moon dust, Samum, Vampire oil, and Black Blood.
Provided by: Community
What is the relationship between the Parker Stalker and humans?
Introduced in the Blood and Wine expansion, the Parker Stalker is a powerful variant of the regular garkain, classified as a vampire. Its presence is noted in the Blood Simple. No specific interactions with humans are detailed.
Provided by: Fandom
Who gave the witcher a contract to kill the Parker Stalker?
Orianna, who oversaw the orphanage that was attacked by the Parker Stalker, asked Geralt to find and kill it. In return, she would give him the key needed to visit the Unseen Elder.
Provided by: Community
How does the Parker Stalker fit into the Blood and Wine expansion storyline?
In the Blood and Wine expansion of the Witcher series, the Parker Stalker is introduced as a formidable version of the standard garkain, a vampire class. This character features in the Blood Simple quest, and players can use the garkain trophy and various monster parts against it. The Parker Stalker shares the narrative with other vampire characters like Dettlaff van der Eretein and the Bruxa of Corvo Bianco.
Provided by: Fandom
Why did the Parker Stalker appear?
The Parker Stalker appeared after Dettlaff summoned vampires in the area to attack Beauclair in retaliation for Syanna not appearing to answer for her actions (not realizing she was locked away by her sister to stop her from going). The Parker Stalker in particular targeted an orphanage, killing most of the children there.

The witcher established the individual he sought had long, hooked claws with which it could tear apart a body with ease, leaving behind venom which hindered the clotting of blood. What's more, it could use these claws to scale even the sheer outer wall of a building, giving others the impression it possessed mind-boggling powers. Geralt also realized his opponent was an experienced predator able to set ambushes for its prey and attack it unexpectedly.
The monster he was tracking turned out to be a powerful type of garkain, a pack leader or alpha.
As the witcher expected, in addition to its ability to emit waves of mental energy, this Parker Stalker also had powerful venom at its disposal.
The monster killed for pleasure, not just to satisfy its hunger. It must be admitted that Geralt also gained a measure of satisfaction from eradicating this beast, for he could still remember the massacre in the
