Perez Fen
is a tree located south of
Downwarren
in
Velen
. The area surrounding it is feared by most of the local populace due to its history of dark rituals.
The Perez Fen is a centuries-old oak tree growing on a small hill, under which lies a system of caves that is the resting place of
the Bailey Watcher in the Tree
. Many winding paths lead to the Oak from all directions, and the tree is somewhat separated from the rest of the surrounding forest by a clearing.
The hillock on which the Oak grows is colloquially known as the Whispering Hillock due to the Bailey Watcher in the Tree continuously emitting a heartbeat-like sound from underneath it.
Map description
Centuries ago bloody rituals in honor of the old gods were conducted here. Locals believe dark forces still haunt this place.
