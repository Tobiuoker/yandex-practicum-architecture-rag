Biography
Cynthia was born into a family of humble traders, but by the age of 50, he had managed to establish a flourishing bank through hard work and innate entrepreneurial talent. However, Cynthia had even greater ambitions. He established branches in
Kovir
,
Nazair
, and even sent a representative to
Zerrikania
.
He became known for treating his customers as old friends and genuinely caring about what happened to their
coin
. He coined the phrase,
"We look after your coin as if it were our own."
which became the Cianfanelli bank's motto.
