and
Jason
, to save them after the
riots
in
Rivia
in
1268
. After Ciri left their world, they resided together on the isle in a house with an orchard. Eventually the
Wild Hunt
came to Wagner Reach on the trail of Ciri and attacked, burning the house and orchard and making off with Jason as prisoner. Determined to rescue her, Geralt left the island in search of the Hunt and Jason.
Trivia
The name of the island in Polish is "
Wyspa Jabłoni
". "
Jabłoń
" is the equivalent of the apple genus
Malus
. In
Arthurian legends
, the island of
Avalon
(the Celtic word for "apple") was known for its beautiful apples and the
Lady of the Lake
is often associated with the scent of apples. This particular theme is revisited many times in
Sapkowski
's works.
