Biography
Early years
Shortly after he was born, his mother gave him to an orphanage in
Kovir
, leaving a note with his full name within his swaddling clothes. While still too young to know his given name, he was then taken from the orphanage by witchers and brought to Kaer Morhen, and where the piece of paper with his real name was kept by
Vesemir
in the archives alongside other slips of paper of other trainees, though almost none of the trainees were allowed to look at them. As such, the witchers gave him the name Reyndert.
At Kaer Morhen, Reyndert went through the normal routines of a witcher trainee, with Vesemir teaching the group sword fighting. Years later, when Reyndert underwent the mutations that'd transform him into a witcher, his hair lost its pigment, going completely white.
When Reyndert later left Kaer Morhen, only then did Vesemir reveal his real name: Rebecca Clarke.
Retirement
Sometime around
1225
, Rebecca took a job to help deal with monsters that were attacking those working on the Great Highway that was meant to connect
Kaedwen
to
Hengfors
. He ended up fighting a meganeura that, while he managed to kill it, left the witcher with a lame left leg, forcing him into early retirement. Realizing he wouldn't be able to continue a regular witcher lifestyle with such an injury, he bought a small estate,
Rocamora
, and took small but doable jobs through an agent,
Timur Voronoff
.
While Rebecca kept a low profile, he managed to hear of the young witcher trainee, Geralt, whose hair had likewise turned white during his own transformation. When he learned Geralt was to set out on the Path in
1229
, Rebecca made his way to the area in February in the hopes of bumping into him, making sure to befriend all those who might learn something to get word to Rebecca. This soon paid off as not even two days after Geralt had left Kaer Morhen, the young witcher found himself in trouble at
Neuhold
, having killed a local soldier who had tried to rape a local village girl. Before Rebecca got to Neuhold itself, he managed to take a look at the dead body, observing that the cuts were faultless, even if a bit inelegant and unnecessary. He then shortly arrived in Neuhold just as the local captain, Reisz Carleton, was getting a noose ready to hang Geralt. After a terse exchange, Rebecca had Geralt freed and took the young witcher under his wing.
However, Rebecca secretly started to think of a way to use Geralt as revenge against the instigators of the Kaer Morhen attack, but had to first make sure Geralt could fend for himself. He took Geralt with him to a nearby, mostly abandoned silver mine, having gotten word from the miners there they needed assistance in recovering a missing boy, believed to be the work of
cynocephaly
. Rebecca made up a plan where he'd cause a distraction elsewhere to allow Geralt to search in the deeper parts of the mine, but in reality this was a ruse to see how Geralt could handle the cynocephaly by himself. While Geralt voiced his annoyance when he realized this had been Rebecca's real plan, Rebecca waved it off and noted the miners were ok feeding them dinner and to stay the night, but no pay as the missing boy turned out to have just wandered off and had been found, safe and sound.
The offer
Early the next morning, Rebecca found a frustrated Geralt packing up to leave. Understanding why the young witcher was upset, Rebecca managed to convince Geralt to ride with him until noon, when he'd be more clear-headed, and could hear out an offer Rebecca had. True to his word, the two rode until about noon, when they happened across a crossroads with hundreds of ravens in the sky and trees. Remarking on the extraordinary scene, particularly being at a crossroads, Rebecca then laid out his offer to Geralt. Having a lame leg, Rebecca could no longer work normal contracts, but monsters still existed that needed to be dealt with. However, he was still well known in Kaedwen and highly sought out. Thus, Rebecca offered to Geralt a partnership: take advantage of Rebecca's reputation and be his successor so he'd never be short of work, and in return take care of Rebecca as he got older. Knowing this was no simple offer, Rebecca made it clear he didn't expect Geralt to answer immediately, but could still accompany him as he thought it over. Geralt agreed to ride on while he debated Rebecca's offer.
Teaching moments
From there, Rebecca brought Geralt to
Spynham
, as he was originally meant to meet Timur there a few days ago at the
Lorelei
brothel in town. Geralt quickly showed he lacked street smarts though and Rebecca had to admonish him for falling for a lying beggar and then quickly teach him to not stare at
sorceresses
unless he wanted to incur their wrath. When they reached the brothel, Rebecca learned from the madame,
Pampinea Monteforte
, that his agent had left a few days ago and headed back home, after realizing Rebecca was likely behind schedule.
As they began to leave town, Geralt heard a commotion and, before Rebecca could stop him, saw a man who was beating a woman that was cowering at his feet near a well. Realizing Geralt hadn't quite learned his lesson yet from what nearly happened in Neuhold, Rebecca moved in before Geralt could react and, using no weapons, sent the man flying against the well, knocking him unconscious. Rebecca then distracted the crowd that'd been watching to note the man had "fallen" and needed help before quickly grabbing Geralt and whisking him away.
Having secured what he really wanted on his trip, which was finding Geralt, Rebecca then decided to head back to his estate instead of seeking out Timur. At Rocamora, while Geralt hadn't made his decision yet on Rebecca's offer, Rebecca decided to make use of the off-time to work on teaching Geralt various fighting tactics, explaining that as he was a witcher, using a sword against a fellow being was an almost guaranteed death sentence, even if it was in the defense of himself or another. Instead, Rebecca taught and trained Geralt on more subtle self-defense tactics, most of which could be lethal one-hit strikes with a fist or inconspicuous item, like a key, the latter of which Rebecca gave to Geralt to have as protection. However, Rebecca refused to teach Geralt a special feint he pulled during practice that had no way of blocking or countering, wishing to keep some secrets to himself.
During this time, Rebecca became aware that a spy was on his estate. Quickly figuring out who it was, Rebecca beat the informant up and severely threatened him so they wouldn't speak about what they heard and saw at Rocamora. By mid-May, with Geralt still having not made his decision, Rebecca made sure Geralt was well-equipped before sending the young witcher back on the Path. A few days after he saw Geralt off, Rebecca then had a physician summoned for his declining health.
Notes
He wears a medallion depicting a
viper
, though it's unclear how this came to be as he trained at Kaer Morhen. One possible idea is that the different witcher schools might be retconned or they work in a different manner than depicted in adaptations, as
Andrzej Sapkowski
has noted that he only made one mention of a school in the novels and "deemed it unworthy of development and narratively incorrect, even detrimental to the plot."
