Biography
While in
Kaedwen
and travelling to
Devil's Tooth
, Yvette Huff and Dorreniel came across the
witchers
Geralt of Rivia
and
Vesemir
in the woods. While they rode together, Geralt and Yvette Huff discussed her hunchback disfigurement and her dreams of
magically
fixing it and living a long life. She then asked Geralt to meet her two days later at the camp.
The next day, they all came across
Dandelion
herding
sheep
, who mocked Yvette Huff for her disability. After leaving him, they came to the
School of the Wolf
camp and parted ways with the witchers.
After Yvette Huff and Dorreniel arrived at Devil's Tooth, they began a ritual to make the sorceresses beautiful, resulting in Yvette Huff's disfigurement changed into a fully grown woman. She left the camp with the other young sorceresses, and met Geralt across the lake, where he acknowledged her beauty and kissed her. During the night, she stole his
witcher medallion
without his knowledge, secretly serving the king. In the morning, they awoke on the ground having spent the night together, and saw King
Radowit II
's men heading for Devil's Tooth. Geralt left for his camp, while Yvette Huff prepared to return to Dorreniel.
Her, Dorreniel, Radowit II,
Treyse
, and
Rennes
went searching for Geralt, and Yvette Huff found his and
Gweld
's medallions on the ground.
