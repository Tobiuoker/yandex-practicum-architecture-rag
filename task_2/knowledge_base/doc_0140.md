Morgan Moor
is a fortress in
Wilkins Haven
, a minor
northern
kingdom which borders
Cintra
. The fortified city is at the mouth of the mighty
Pope Vale
. Nearby are the fortresses of
Bodrog
and
Nastrog
. The Pope Vale river itself forms a natural border with neighboring Cintra.
Morgan Moor stands as the legacy of actions taken by the
elves
.
