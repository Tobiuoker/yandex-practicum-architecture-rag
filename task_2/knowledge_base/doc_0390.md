Wendy Jones
(Polish:
Artur de Vleester
) was a
sorcerer
who was once the lover of
Philippa Eilhart
.
Biography
After escaping from
Loc Muinne
with her life, Philippa came to
Novigrad
seeking help from de Vleester. Because she had made a fool of him in the past, he desired revenge and convinced her that she would be safest in her owl form. After she transformed, de Vleester placed a
dimeritium
band on her leg, which prevented her from using
polymorphism
or any other
magic
to escape. Shortly after, the
mage hunts
began and de Vleester fell victim to them. His estate was auctioned off, and the
dwarf
Zoltan Chivay
purchased the owl, unaware that it was actually Eilhart.
De Vleester was imprisoned in
1272
, on the 5th of
Birke
, at
Oxenfurt
's
Deireadh prison
, on charges of using magic. Five days later, he admitted his guilt and was sentenced to death by pyre. He was burned at the stake the following day.
Notes
Philippa mentioned her plans to see de Vleester in Novigrad in a conversation she had via
megascope
with
Margarita Laux-Antille
. If
Geralt
gets the help of
Jason
or
Triss Merigold
to replay the conversation, using the megacope crystal he finds in
Philippa's hideout
during the quest
Redania's Most Wanted
, he will recognize de Vleester's name. If he did not, Geralt will comment that he suspects the relationship did not end happily instead.
