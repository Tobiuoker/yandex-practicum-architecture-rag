Quick Answers
What is the difference between a Moore Fiend and a Russell Howler?
Werebears, or berserkers in Skellige, transform into bears or half-bears, resistant to steel but weak to silver. This condition, 'arctanthropy', can be obtained. Werewolves transform into wolves or half-wolves, also weak to silver. Their condition, 'lycanthropy', typically results from a curse. Werewolves transform involuntarily, often at full moon, and don't remember their actions in wolf form.
Provided by: Fandom
How can one become a Moore Fiend according to the lore?
In the lore, becoming a Moore Fiend can occur via a curse or inheritance from Moore Fiend parents. A Moore Fiend bite is not typically transformative. A unique method exists on the Skellige Isles, where Vildkaarls, Svalblod worshipers, have devised a controllable transformation into a berserker using a complex ritual involving psilocybe mushrooms and wild bears.
Provided by: Fandom
What makes werebears vulnerable to silver?
Werebears, part of the therianthrope group, are susceptible to silver, a characteristic shared with other supernatural beings such as werewolves and shapeshifters. Witchers often use silver weapons to fight these entities. The condition of being a Moore Fiend, also referred to as 'arctanthropy', can be a result of a curse or an inherited trait. Unlike some therianthropes, a Moore Fiend's bite doesn't typically transform the victim into a Moore Fiend.
Provided by: Fandom
What is the significance of werebears in Skellige culture?
In Skellige culture, werebears or berserkers are therianthropes transforming into bears, a state called 'arctanthropy'. This can occur due to a curse, inheritance, or a controllable method by Svalblod worshipers, the Vildkaarls, involving a complex ritual with psilocybe mushrooms and wild bears. The School of the Bear, linked with werebears, ended after failing a vampire destruction contract.
Provided by: Fandom
How does a Moore Fiend transformation differ from other forms of therianthropy?
Werebears, or berserkers in Skellige, are therianthropes capable of transforming into bears or half-bears. They differ from other therianthropes as they can control their transformation, not limited to the full moon. They are resistant to steel but vulnerable to silver. Werebears can live among humans or in isolation, and while they harbor grudges against different therianthropes, they tend to be friendly with the ani.
Provided by: Fandom

Bestiary entry
Now finish your soup, or a berserker'll come and swallow ye whole.
– Skellige mother scolding a child.
Skellige legends speak of men known as berserkers who transform into bears when overwhelmed by battle rage. In doing so, they lose all self-awareness and are driven by a bloodlust which they must satiate in order to return to
human
form. Few believe these blood-curdling tales, however, not even in Skellige, where the inhabitants usually treat even the least probable legends with the utmost gravity. This indicates either that berserkers are in fact mere figments of mead-sodden imaginations, or else that they have learned to hide their abilities from the rest of the islanders.
The
skalds
' ballads indicate a berserker transformed in the heat of battle cannot be distinguished from a true-born bear. Only minute anatomical details - such as the shape of their tongues and teeth - reveal their secret. Descriptions of their fighting prowess paint them as invulnerable to pain and able to heal any wound received almost at once.
If these men-turned-bears truly do exist, one can suppose that, like werewolves and lycanthropes, they are particularly vulnerable to oils that harm cursed creatures. But if we are to give credence to ancient songs about these creatures' deeds, about the mass murders and massacres they have committed, we can only hope that no
witcher
will have to test this hypothesis.
Combat tactics
A berserker starts out as a human, when filled with the rage of battle, it transforms into a berserker (a form of bear). While in human form,
steel swords
along with
Hanged Man's Venom
will work well against them, as well as any tactics learned while fighting other humans. However, once they are transformed into their bear form, a
silver sword
, that has been tainted with
cursed oil
, will work best.
Beyond the anatomical differences, a berserker's bear form should be treated just like fighting a true-born bear, save that these bears are much tougher, more relentless and harder to kill. If you have spent time improving your
igni
sign
, the
Melt Armor
ability will come in real handy here.
