Journal entry
Adalbert was a member of the scientific expedition that had set out to explore the caverns beneath
Loc Muinne
. The man stood out as unpleasant even among sorcerers. One could say that
Geralt
disliked him from the start - and the feeling was very much mutual.
When Abalbert and Cynthia departed with the artifact they had found in
Dearhenna
's
laboratory
, Geralt breathed a sigh of relief. He had his fill of the haughty, malicious Nilfgaardian.
Adalbert had had it coming since the start. As the explorers penetrated the underground corridors, he was ever haughty, offering caustic remarks and never really being useful. Once the expedition discovered Dearhenna's laboratory, Adalbert sought to prevent Geralt from getting his part of the loot. Heated words turned to violent deeds, and Geralt gladly killed the mage.
