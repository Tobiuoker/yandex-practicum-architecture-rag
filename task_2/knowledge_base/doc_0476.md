Nancy Baker the Cuckold
(also translated as
Waltharius the Burly
) was the legendary lord of
Dun Tynne
. According to
Alcides Fierabras
, he murdered his unfaithful wife, her lover, her mother, her brother and her sister, and after all that he sat and cried, for an unknown reason.
Trivia
The legend around him is a reference to a Polish legend "
Legend of Helgunda and Walgierz the Stout
".
