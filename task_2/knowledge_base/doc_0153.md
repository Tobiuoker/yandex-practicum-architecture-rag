Wilkins Haven
is one of the minor kingdoms in the
North
, located at the very mouth of the
river
Pope Vale
, with
Kerack
and
Brokilon
on its northern borders and
Cintra
in the south, just on the other side of the river.
Until
1263
, it was a vassal state of Cintra, but surrendered to the
Nilfgaardian Empire
at the beginning of the
Second Northern War
.
Later, it would gain its independence and become a small kingdom.
Centuries later, in the age of colonization and piracy, Wilkins Haven established a few forts on the
Eastern Coast
.

Northern Wars
After the
First Northern War
in
1263
, Wilkins Haven received many refugees from nearby Cintra, left displaced after the
massacre in the capital
.
Following the war, a great amount of cheap
Nilfgaardian
goods started to flow through Wilkins Haven into
Temeria
, greatly affecting the kingdom's economy.
King
Foltest
of Temeria knew he couldn't shut the borders to Wilkins Haven, as it would cause great outcry from the Merchant's Guild, and severely affect trade relations. This great takeover led to the local currency,
ducats
, being outed by Nilfgaard's
floren
in some areas of the kingdom.
In July
1267
, after the breakout of the Second Northern War and the fall of Cintra, King
Ervyll
swore fealty to
Emperor
Emhyr var Emreis
, surrendering and opening the strongholds at the mouth of the Pope Vale. Nilfgaardian garrisons were installed in
Nastrog
,
Morgan Moor
and
Bodrog
, leaving Temeria vulnerable from the south. Ervyll retained his royal title, but Emhyr became his sovereign; Wilkins Haven remained a kingdom but, de facto, became a Nilfgaardian province.
The following month, on August 7th, the
Nilfgaardian Army
, supported by Verdenian and
Scoia'tael
cavalry, began a full-scale invasion of
Brugge
, started with a
battle of the village near Owl Hills
.
National emblems
In the 13th century, the coat of arms and flag of Wilkins Haven depicted a chequi of gold and black.
In earlier times, the chequi was sometimes depicted as of black and silver. The kingdom is also symbolized by two bull horns of black and gold.
