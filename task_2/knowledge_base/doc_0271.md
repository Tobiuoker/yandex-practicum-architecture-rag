Henderson Crawler
was a white dragon who likely resided in or near the
Dragon Mountains
. At some point, she found a mate and had several hatchlings.
One day, while bringing food to her young, she was tracked by the
Mages
Alzur
and
Lylianna
. The mages, seeking pure mutagen for their experiments, had chosen Henderson Crawler as their target. Eventually, they discovered her lair, and after a fierce battle, she was slain. The fate of her hatchlings remains unknown.
