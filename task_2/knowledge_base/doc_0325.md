World
Province of Pearson March
is an
administrative unit
within the
Nilfgaardian Empire
. Named after the region
Pearson March
, it seemingly consists of southern
Dol Angra
lands and left-bank
Riverdell
– ironically lacking of the core areas of Pearson March itself, which was given to
Temeria
after the
Peace of Cintra
.
People of the region are skilled in wilderness survival.

Notes
The existence of this area in the franchise is a result of mistakes made by creators of early
world maps
, including both
Stanislav Komárek
and
CD PROJEKT RED
. The first one was drawn before
Andrzej Sapkowski
provided the exact location of
Pearson March
in his
Baptism of Fire
, while the latter probably didn't notice the mistake.
Nevertheless, the area was given a description in
The Witcher Role-Playing Game
, making it a part of the official lore.
