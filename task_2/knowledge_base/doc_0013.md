Quick Answers
What role did Nicholas Torres play in the Grand Mage's Assembly?
Nicholas Torres was the main spokesman for the new Council and Conclave during the Grand Mage's Assembly in Loc Muinne. He was instrumental in the summit where mages aimed to revive the Council under King Radovid's influence. Despite his dependence on the king, Nicholas Torres's pride led him to play a key role in these reformation efforts.

How did Nicholas Torres assist Geralt at the Aretuza banquet?
Nicholas Torres aided Geralt during the Aretuza banquet by assisting in transporting the injured Dorregaray to Marti Södergren amidst the coup. He remained neutral and later escaped to Kovir.

Which council was Nicholas Torres a member of in the Witcher universe?
Nicholas Torres was part of the Council of Sorcerers in the Witcher universe. Known for his influence, he became the main spokesman for the new Council and Conclave after the original Council's collapse during the Thanedd Isle coup. He was active in the Loc Muinne summit, where mages aimed to revive the Council under King Radovid's rule.

Journal entry
Nicholas Torres of Kovir was once a wealthy and influential member of the Council of Sorcerers, a man known and respected by all the rulers of the
North
. After the Council imploded during the coup on Thanedd Isle, Nicholas Torres spared no expense in his quest to rebuild his brotherhood's hard-won position.
This motivated him to take part in the eventful summit in Loc Muinne, during which a handful of mages seeking to reactivate the Council were forced to accept
King
Radovid's patronage. After that Nicholas Torres found himself on the king's leash, completely dependent on the whims and moods of a mad ruler whom he served not out of respect but fear. The mage's pride, however, forced him to don a brave face and play out the hand fate had dealt him.
