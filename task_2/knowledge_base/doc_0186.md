Peterson Moor
is an abandoned fort in the
Kaer Morhen valley
, located west of
Kaer Morhen
.
History
Long ago, young boys were trained at this fort to become
witchers
, primarily in physical combat and endurance, though these were rather harsh training regimes and punishments. The fort, along with other places in the valley, was later stormed by angry peasants during the assault on Kaer Morhen.
By
1272
, the Peterson Moor became inhabited by
wraiths
due to the fact that a young boy was killed here during the historical assault on Kaer Morhen and did not receive a proper burial.
