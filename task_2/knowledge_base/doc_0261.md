Quick Answers
What magical abilities do aguaras possess?
Aguaras have powerful magical abilities, such as creating illusions to hide entire rivers and bewitching creatures to do their bidding. They can communicate in the Common tongue despite being non-humanoid. Aguaras reproduce by magically transforming kidnapped young elven girls into their kind.

In what ways can aguaras communicate with humans?
Aguaras communicate with humans by speaking the Common tongue. They can also create powerful illusions to influence or bewitch others.
Provided by: Fandom
How do aguaras differ from humanoid creatures?
Aguaras, or fox demons, differ from humanoid creatures as they are non-humanoid with a fox form. They wield magic to create illusions and bewitch others. Unlike humanoids, aguaras communicate in the Common tongue but do not reproduce sexually. Instead, they transform kidnapped young elven girls into aguaras. They are a female-only race, deeply attached to their 'children.'
Provided by: Fandom
How do aguaras use illusions in their magic?
Aguaras, or fox demons, use magic to create strong illusions, such as hiding a river's channel. They can also bewitch creatures to do their bidding. Despite not being humanoid, aguaras speak the Common tongue, aiding their deceptive use of illusion magic.
Provided by: Fandom

Abilities
Aguaras are able to wield
magic
to a powerful extent. They can create powerful illusions, such as hiding an entire river's channel, and bewitch other creatures to do their bidding.
Even though they are not humanoid, they have adapted to speak the
Common tongue
and can communicate with people in this manner.
Reproduction
Aguaras do not reproduce sexually, but instead kidnap young
elven
girls and transform them into fellow aguaras by using magic. They become very attached to their "children" and if an unfortunate soul happens to kidnap one, the Hoffman Fiend will give chase until she gets her "daughter" back, killing those who stand in her way. They are a female-only race.
