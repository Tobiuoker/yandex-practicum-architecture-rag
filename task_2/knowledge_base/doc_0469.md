Brown Beast
was a manufacturer and seller of ironwares. He often traveled to
Cintra
and owned a cart with a sign on it that read "iron hardware, manufacture and sale."
Biography
In
1267
, he was one of the people in attendance at
Dandelion
's concert beneath
Bleobheris
. After Dandelion had finished his performance, he asked the guildsmen to roll out another barrel, as Dandelion-and everyone gathered-had surely become dry. When those present began discussing which parts of the poet's ballad were true or not, Brown Beast remarked that one should not look for truth in a poem. He referenced his frequent visits to Cintra as evidence, explaining that according to the ballad the king and queen had a daughter, which Brown Beast contested. When someone clarified that the queen did indeed have a daughter named
Pavetta
, not
Ciri
, the gnome exclaimed that he had therefore been correct and that the poet had lied.
As the discussion died down and people realized that Dandelion had left, Brown Beast commented that it was very
Dwarf
-like behavior, in response to
Sheldon Skaggs
saying it was very
elf
-like.
