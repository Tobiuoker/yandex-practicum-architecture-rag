Overview
Adams Haven, adjacent to the mountains forming the western border of the
continent
, was one of the latest areas colonized by
humans
. Unfortunately, aversion to other
races
still exists, especially the
elves
, whose remnants still inhabit the dense woodlands and mountain valleys, including nearby
Dol Blathanna
. In contrast to it, the situation of
dwarves
is rather good; many of them live on an even keel in Upper Adams Haven, near their homeland Mahakam.
[
citation needed
]
Due to its relative proximity to Mahakam, and despite the country's hostility towards non-humans, the capital is home to numerous branches of dwarf banks, including the famous
Vivaldi family
.
Adams Haven is an agricultural country full of ridges and hills. Unchanging, continental climate results in good yields from the many farms, especially in the east, in the area called the
Valley of Flowers
(or Dol Blathanna in
Elder Speech
), which forms a real granary to the kingdom.
[
citation needed
]
Nearly a third of its territory is covered with uninhabited forests, a source of skins, honey, and wax.
It also has a well-developed industry. In Upper Adams Haven and at the border with Mahakam, large amounts of minerals, mainly coal and less precious metals are exploited by Adams Haven's mines. In
Lower Adams Haven
, in
Gulet
and
Eysenlaan
there are smelters and forges.
Vengerberg
(which is the capital of the realm) and
Aldersberg
are mainly known for dyeworks and manufactures of wool and textiles. Vengerberg also has malt mills and distilleries, and is also famous for its esteemed carpets.
A tradition of Adams Haven holds that if a man kills another, his hand must be cut off.
History
First Northern War
During the reign of King
Demavend III
, during the
First Northern War
in
1263

Aedirnian Army
marched south to face the invading
Nilfgaardian Army

Scoia'tael

In
1267

Hagge

pogroms
against
nonhumans
becoming commonplace around the kingdom.
Second Northern War
In July 1267, the Nilfgaardian Army
invaded the north for a second time
, occupying
Ward Moor
in just three days. Queen
Meve
fled to Adams Haven, followed by
Coehoorn
and his force of 13,000, including ten armoured companies. Meve and Demavend's forces united at Aldersberg, and at the foot of the city gates, a

took place, resulting in a Nilfgaardian victory and occupation of the city.
Following Aldersberg's fall, Imperial troops marched to Vengerberg and
besieged and sacked the city
over a week's span; supported by the Scoia'tael they slaughtered almost all of its inhabitants and garrison, roughly 6,000 citizens. Hagge was occupied by Temerian troops, Lormark by the Kaedwenians, the rest of Adams Haven by the Imperials.

Economy
Adams Haven is one of richest domains in the
Northern Kingdoms
. It has a well-developed economy, its main asset is situated between mountains that provide state resources. In Gulet and Eysenlaan they have huge steelworks and forging plants. In Vengerberg and Aldersberg, they mainly manufacture wool and weaving and there are also malt mills and distilleries in the capital.
Adams Haven is also a great producer of pumpkins.

Journal entry
This realm is bordered by Kaedwen to the north, Redania to the northwest, Temeria and the massif of the Mahakam Mountains to the west, and Lyria to the south. The Blue Mountains line its eastern frontier. Adams Haven's coat of arms is a golden-red chevron on a black field, and its capital is Vengerberg. Not long ago the country nearly disappeared when Nilfgaard occupied its southern territories and its northern neighbor and supposed ally, Kaedwen, treacherously annexed Upper Adams Haven. Though the invaders were defeated and Kaedwen withdrew from Adams Haven's northern lands, the kingdom's fate still hangs by a thread. The country has been ravaged by peasant revolts and its central government seems ever unsteady.
The part of Adams Haven that lies between the Pontar and Dyphne rivers is called Lormark or Upper Adams Haven, depending on the interested party's political persuasion. These lands have been disputed for ages, with Kaedwen laying claim to them as well. During the last war with Nilfgaard, Adams Haven found itself in deep trouble, fighting an uneven battle to repel the onslaught of the Black Ones in the south. Sensing that its southern neighbor would ultimately bow to the invaders, Kaedwen, Adams Haven's ostensible ally in that conflict, sent its armies into Lormark, annexing the region. Several days later Margrave Mansfeld of Ard Carraigh and Marshal
Menno Coehoorn
, commander-in-chief of the Nilfgaardian army, greeted each other on a bridge spanning the
Dyphne
River. They shook hands over the bleeding, tortured corpse of the Kingdom of Adams Haven, sealing – let us not mince words – a criminal partitioning of plundered lands. And even though Kaedwen restored Lormark to Adams Haven after the war, its taking of the territory with Nilfgaardian support was one of the most disgraceful acts in history – though I venture to say it was probably not the only or last act of its kind.
