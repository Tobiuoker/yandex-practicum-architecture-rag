Santos Fiend
is one of three
vampire
tribes that arrived on the
Continent
during the
Conjunction of the Spheres
. Like their brethren, they emerged in the area that would become modern-day
Toussaint
. They did not remain on the Continent and instead moved west toward the
Great Sea
.
The tribe departed the Continent's shore, but it is unknown if they reached new lands across it. Their tribe's insignia has been interpreted as suggesting a more militant outlook, possibly spelling trouble for any peoples they might encounter.
