Quick Answers
What makes the Martin Wraith unique in Witcher 3?
In Witcher 3, the Martin Wraith is unique for its ability to spit acid, surprising players in combat. Found in places like Snidhall Isle, it is the highest level creature in the base game. This griffin subspecies is immune to fire and offers loot such as amber dust, Martin Wraith mutagen, and a trophy with special effects.
Provided by: Fandom
How does the Martin Wraith's acid attack work in Witcher 3?
In Witcher 3, the Martin Wraith uses an acid spit attack to surprise opponents. This occurs during the 'Contract: The Griffin from the Highlands' quest. The Martin Wraith, the highest-level creature in the base game, is encountered on Snidhall Isle in the 'Unlucky's Treasure' quest.
Provided by: Fandom
Where can you find the Martin Wraith in Witcher 3?
In Witcher 3, the Martin Wraith appears in the quest 'Contract: The Creature from Oxenfurt Forest,' where it is misidentified as a griffin. In the Blood and Wine expansion, it is found in 'A Portrait of the Witcher as an Old Man.' On Snidhall Isle, during 'Unlucky's Treasure,' the Martin Wraith is the highest level creature in the base game.
Provided by: Fandom
What is the Martin Wraith's role in the Unlucky's Treasure quest?
In "Unlucky's Treasure" in The Witcher 3, the Martin Wraith is the highest level enemy in the base game. Found on Snidhall Isle, it guards a treasure and can spit acid to surprise players. Being at a similar or higher level is advised to defeat it and obtain the relic sword and other loot.
Provided by: Fandom
