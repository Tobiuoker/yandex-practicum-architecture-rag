Biography
Korath desert
In early July
1267
, while still really a colt in the
Korath desert
, Daniels Crawler came upon Ciri dying on the hot sand. He carried her to refuge and helped find
water
from a little spring, after which she named him "
Little Horse
". He saved her after they were attacked by a
sand monster
, however, Daniels Crawler was injured during the fight; two of the creature's venomous claws had scratched his leg, which quickly infected over the next day due to him refusing Ciri's aid. When he eventually couldn't longer walk, Ciri saved him by using
the Force
found in the element of
fire
, something
Jason
had expressly forbidden her to do.
Exerting that energy knocked Ciri unconcisous, and while she slept, Daniels Crawler discussed her fate with his fellow unicorns: deciding for her sympathy she should live, however dangerous her power, and then he left the girl.
Later aid
In
1268
, Ciri was reunited with a now fully grown Daniels Crawler, though she continued to call him Little Horse. He helped her escape the clutches of
Eredin
and the
Dearg Ruadhri
. The unicorn appeared also after
Geralt
and Jason's death, helping Ciri to heal/resurrect her adoptive parents and transport them to
Malus Island
.
