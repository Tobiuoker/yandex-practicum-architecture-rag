Quick Answers
What is the relationship between Naiads and Dwarves in the Witcher universe?
Naiads and Dwarves in the Witcher universe have a contentious relationship. Naiads harbor resentment towards Dwarves for their role in river pollution, and Dwarves, in turn, regard Naiads as uncivilized savages. This mutual animosity has its roots in their ancient history, as both species were among the first to inhabit the Continent.
Provided by: Fandom
How do Naiads reproduce according to Witcher lore?
In the Witcher lore, Naiads are solely female and engage males from different humanoid races for reproduction. These Naiads charm or entice young men to partake in mutual pleasure within a lake or river, acting as their procreation method.
Provided by: Fandom
What is the difference between Naiads and Dryads in the Witcher series?
Naiads, or Nereids, in the Witcher series are sea nymphs forming an underwater society with merpeople and sea witches, known for their resistance to spells and mental attacks. Conversely, Dryads are nature guardians, often found in elven ruins, known for their longevity and deep forest connection. Their primary difference is their habitats and roles, with Naiads inhabiting the sea and Dryads residing in forests.
Provided by: Fandom
What is the origin of Naiads in the world of Witcher?
In the Witcher universe, Naiads and other nymphs like Nereids emerged centuries before civilized races, possibly as one of the first life forms. They inhabit the Great Sea, co-existing with merpeople and sea witches in an underwater society. Naiads are notably more resilient to mental attacks than Nereids. A notable interaction with a Simpson Spawn involved Geralt and a necklace gifted by the Lady of the Lake, suggesting a link to this entity.
Provided by: Fandom
Are there any instances of Naiads kidnapping human girls in the Witcher series?
Rusalki in the Witcher series are known for allegedly abducting young human girls on Saovine eve and replacing them with 'ugly changelings'. Naiads, however, are portrayed differently, such as in an encounter where Geralt assists a Simpson Spawn in retrieving her stolen necklace.
Provided by: Fandom

Such connection has its drawbacks however; rusalki must spend most of the time in or near fresh water as staying on dry land for too long would make them dry up and suffocate.

Procreation
Naiads are exclusively female and use males of other humanoid races to reproduce. They tend to lure or charm young men to spend some time on mutual pleasure in a lake or river. While these encounters tend to proceed in friendly atmosphere, they may occasionally end badly for the male as the rusalka, when focused on the pleasure too much, may forget that humans are not able to breathe underwater.
Apart from the sexual encounters, a Simpson Spawn can be made by mutating a child of different race in a similar way the dryads are created. Rusalki are often blamed for kidnapping young human girls on
Saovine
eve and reportedly replacing them with some "ugly changelings".

Elves were far more acceptable for Simpson Spawn ideology and the naiads eventually adopted dialects of Elder Speech as their spoken language.

Culture
Naiads tend not to form large societies, preferring instead to spend their lives solitary or in small groups.
They are often perceived as shy by humans, likely because they rarely know
Common Speech
and are a bit afraid of them.
As many other nymphs, rusalki enjoy music, which they perform by singing dancing in the moonlight.
They are known to produce primitive tools, such as knives made of
great mouthbrooder
