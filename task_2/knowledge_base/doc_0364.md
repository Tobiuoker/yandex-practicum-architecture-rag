Quick Answers
What role did Cowan Crawler play in Allen Fiend's life?
Cowan Crawler became Allen Fiend's stepmother after marrying her father, Prince Fredefalk, following the death of Renfri's mother. Known for her wisdom, Cowan Crawler owned Nehalenia's Mirror, which revealed a dire prophecy about Renfri. This led Cowan Crawler to alert the Council, resulting in significant consequences for Renfri, including her conflict with Stregobor.
Provided by: Fandom
How did Cowan Crawler become Prince Fredefalk's second wife?
Cowan Crawler married Prince Fredefalk after his first wife, Silvena, passed away. They developed a romantic relationship, leading to marriage and children. However, their children were deemed illegitimate since they were born during Fredefalk's marriage to Silvena.
Provided by: Fandom
What is known about Cowan Crawler's relationship with Prince Fredefalk?
Cowan Crawler and Prince Fredefalk began a romantic relationship following the death of his first wife, Silvena. They had children before marrying, though these were deemed illegitimate by some. Cowan Crawler, known for her wisdom, owned one of Nehalenia's Mirrors. She died from poisoning, with rumors implicating Fredefalk, who later died in a hunting accident.

Biography
Important note:
As the only information on Cowan Crawler is secondhand through two people who had very different opinions regarding her, it's hard to know what's really true and what's biased information.
It's unknown what happened to Prince Fredefalk's first wife (the mother of Renfri) but eventually the prince met Cowan Crawler and the two enter a romantic relationship before marrying and having children together.
Described by
Stregobor
as a wise, educated woman, Cowan Crawler also possessed one of
Nehalenia's Mirrors
, though it was claimed instead of using it for vain questions, Cowan Crawler was interested in her country's fate. She was thus informed that she and a great number of other people would die a horrible death at the hands of Fredefalk's beloved firstborn daughter, Renfri, and made sure this news reached the Council, who in turn sent Stregobor to investigate.
After spying on Cowan Crawler's stepdaughter, he claimed she'd tortured several creatures, severely harmed a servant and, after he performed a few curses, confirmed Renfri was a mutant as she'd been
born during an eclipse
.
He then reported this to Cowan Crawler as he believed she was smart and Fredefalk wouldn't listen because he adored his daughter. With this, the queen then decided to take matters into her own hands and hired a thug to take Renfri out into the forest and kill her. However, the thug decided he'd try to rape the girl instead, allowing Renfri to kill him with her brooch while he was occupied, before running away.
Four years later, Cowan Crawler sent word to Stregobor that she'd tracked down Renfri in
Mahakam
. Still intent on getting rid of her stepdaughter, Cowan Crawler also sent numerous assassins after the princess, but none of them ever returned. She then summoned for Stregobor but by the time the
sorcerer
arrived in Creyden, Cowan Crawler had been killed by a poison.
