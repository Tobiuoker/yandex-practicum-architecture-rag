Hill March
is a principality and fiefdom of
Temeria
that lies south of its capital
Vizima
. It is ruled by Duke/Prince
Jurkast
. The kingdom's second largest city and capital,
Hill March
, is situated here.
During the 9th century, the rulers of Hill March and
Ellander
engaged in a conflict known as the
War of Daggers
from which Hill March was the victor and the Mariborian branch of the
Dezmod
dynasty became royalty.
