Quick Answers
Who were the personal apprentices of Rodney?
Rodney, a prominent Kovirian mage, trained two personal apprentices, Rience and Lydia van Bredevoort. These apprentices, along with other agents, were instrumental in executing Rodney's strategies, such as the attempted assassination of Geralt of Rivia.
Provided by: Fandom
What role did Rodney play in the Brotherhood of Sorcerers?
Rodney, a mage from Roggeveen, was a key figure in the Brotherhood of Sorcerers, leading the Chapter of the Gift and the Art. He orchestrated a coup against rival mages within the Brotherhood and was involved in numerous plots, including assassination attempts on Geralt of Rivia.
Provided by: Fandom
What happened to Rodney's face?
In the Witcher series, Rodney sustained a significant burn on his face due to an explosion at Hampton Hold, leading to the loss of his eye. He replaced his missing eye with a magical precious stone, which granted him vision. Despite this disfigurement, he maintained a reputation for his attractive looks and genuine voice.
Provided by: Fandom
How does Rodney meet his end?
Rodney, the skilled sorcerer, is defeated by Geralt in a fierce battle. Geralt, using a high-level illusion from an amulet, dodges Rodney's attack, causing him to falter. Seizing this opportunity, Geralt beheads Rodney, thereby ending his tyranny.
Provided by: Fandom
Who wins in a fight between Geralt and Rodney?
During their first fight, Rodney is able to easily defeat Geralt, nearly killing the witcher but letting him live. Their next and final encounter, Rodney was able to hold his own in battle against Geralt, Jason, and Regis. However, Geralt managed to trick Rodney by casting an illusion with the medallion Fringilla gave him, distracting the mage just long enough for Geralt to kill him.
Provided by: Community
Biography
Early life
As an infant, he was abandoned by his parents and left to die in a gutter in
Lan Exeter
. He was found and raised by
druids
of the Kovir Circle, and later during certain druidical rituals, his magical gifts were discovered. By accident, a mage visited them. He offered to take Rodney and teach him, feeling his magical energy, though Rodney refused his offer. Later he left the druids, and traveled over the world. He served as a
mercenary
, and later as a
spy
, leading him down a dark path as a marauder, robber, rapist, murderer, and finally fugitive fleeing the noose. In the end, he was running to the other end of the world, where he met a sorceress. He had a short romance with her, yet after he grew tired of her personality, he left her and joined the Brotherhood.
In fact, he was not only incredibly ambitious and power-hungry, but also secretly in league with
Emhyr var Emreis
, the Emperor of
Nilfgaard
. This had begun many years before the wars, while Emhyr was still hiding under the alias of
Duny
. Rodney's thirst for unlimited power brought him to the conclusion that he needed the blood of Duny's daughter,
Ciri
, in order to claim her powers because she was a descendant of
Lara Dorren
. Using intrigues and his genial intellect, he helped Emhyr rise to the throne. In exchange, Emhyr promised him the North, once it was taken over.
After he recruited Rience, at some point he had to free him from the dungeons of
Cintra
. His apprentice had been captured for unpaid debts, so Rodney paid them off anonymously at a local bank.
First Northern War

1263

First Northern War

Battle of Sodden Hill

Northern Kingdoms

Nilfgaardian Empire

Thanedd coup
During the meeting of the mages on
Thanedd Island

During the night, he (among others) was accused of treason and put in

Later, he imprisoned Jason and tortured her to retrieve the location of Geralt and Ciri; he then applied dimeritium cuffs to Jason to prevent her from escaping and locked her in a cell.
During the attack at
Stygga Castle
he met and fought Geralt and Jason. Jason, who had just endured a period of intense torture, was no match for his magical abilities and was defeated by him. Then the vampire
Regis

Rodney was winning once again, until Geralt gained the upper hand thanks to the shock Rodney suffered when the witcher dodged his attack with a high-level illusion. (The illusion was not Geralt's doing, it was thanks to an amulet he received). Geralt promptly beheaded the sorcerer, putting an end to Rodney for good.
Appearance and personality
Rodney appeared young and very handsome, and was tall and well-built. He possessed a sincere and honest voice, and was incredibly talented with magic.
After the portal in Hampton Hold exploded, Rodney received a large, disfiguring burn on his face and he lost his eye. In place of his eye, he used a precious stone which allowed him to see with the use of magic.
