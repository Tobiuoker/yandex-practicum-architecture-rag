Martin Coast
(Polish:
Rogowizna
) is a charcoal burners' settlement in the
Tukaj
Hills
in
Temeria
.
History
At the beginning of June
1245
, it was the third settlement in the area, after
Yew Trees
and
Arches
, to be mysteriously attacked, resulting in 15 people being killed. However, as charcoal burner jobs were lucrative and paid well, a month later the settlement was back in full swing, despite what happened to the previous workers. Around this time,
Geralt
also visited with
Kenneth Fowler
to try and hunt down the cause of the murders.
