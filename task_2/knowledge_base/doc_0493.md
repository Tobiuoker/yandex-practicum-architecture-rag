Quick Answers
Who was Jones Watcher before she became a nightwraith?
Before becoming a nightwraith, Jones Watcher was Christine Hensley, a woman from Midcopse, Velen. Her transformation occurred post her murder by a villager, Bokhai, whom she had rejected.
Provided by: Fandom
What led to Christine Hensley's transformation into Jones Watcher?
Christine Hensley, who lived in Midcopse, planned to escape with her partner, Ontan, due to the frightening advances of another villager, Bokhai. Upon discovering their plan, Bokhai pursued and killed Christine Hensley, causing her transformation into the nightwraith, Jones Watcher.
Provided by: Fandom
Who was the person responsible for Christine Hensley's death?
In 1272, Christine Hensley, a resident of Midcopse, was murdered by Bokhai, a fellow villager. Bokhai's jealousy and resentment stemmed from Christine Hensley's rejection of him due to her relationship with Ontan. Upon learning of Christine Hensley and Ontan's plan to escape, Bokhai pursued them and ended up killing Christine Hensley.
Provided by: Fandom
What is the relationship between Christine Hensley and Ontan?
Christine Hensley and Ontan, residents of Midcopse in 1272, were involved in a romantic relationship. Their bond was disrupted by Bokhai, a villager who harbored unrequited feelings for Christine Hensley and persistently pursued her. The tension culminated in Christine Hensley's tragic demise, caused by Bokhai.
Provided by: Fandom
How can Jones Watcher be defeated in Witcher 3?
In Witcher 3, Geralt can defeat Jones Watcher by using the dagger that killed Christine Hensley. This action summons the wraith, which Geralt must then swiftly eliminate to prevent her from regaining health. After the fight, Geralt can acquire the nightwraith trophy and Forgotten Vran Sword from her remains and claim his reward from the ealdorman, thereby completing the quest.
Provided by: Fandom

So... That's to mean... our Christine Hensley's the wraith?
–
Bolko
, ealdorman of Midcopse
It is said true love's flame is never extinguished. This sad truth is the reason why Christine Hensley of Midcopse, whom an early death had separated from her beloved, was unable to find peace in the next life, and instead returned to haunt her former environs as a nightwraith.
Fighting such an apparition is extremely difficult. A nightwraith will form mirror images of herself to confuse her opponent and aid her in battle. She herself can take on immaterial form, rendering her invulnerable to blows. The best way to force her out of this state is to set a trap with the
Yrden
Sign, then quickly followup with
Igni
while she is caught. Most important of all, however, is this: never attempt to fight one in the middle of the night, when the moon hangs high in the sky.
This vengeful
wraith
might very well have killed every last inhabitant of the village, had not a famous witcher,
Geralt of Rivia
, appeared in
Velen
- and been in need of a bit of coin.
