# Johnson Blade

Johnson Blade
is a
light
armor
in
The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt
that comes in three forms:
Johnson Blade (version 1)
Johnson Blade (version 2)
Johnson Blade (version 3)
You can acquire all 3 doublets at the beginning of the main quest
Imperial Audience
, when told to get dressed by the chamberlain
Mererid
. If kept, they can later be used for attending banquets during other quests:
A Matter of Life and Death
The King is Dead – Long Live the King
The Man from Cintra
They're also sold by
Elihal
, and in the
Blood and Wine
expansion by
Pierre
as well.
