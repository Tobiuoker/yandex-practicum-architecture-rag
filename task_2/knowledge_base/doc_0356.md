Overview
Both polar ice caps of the planet cover larger surface than those of
Old Earth
.
Brown Vale surrounds the North Pole and borders
Far North
's tundras and northernmost mountain chains in the south; venturing from the Pole into another direction would perhaps lead one into foreign lands unknown to the
Nordlings
, albeit even the
Mayo Howler
who inhabit the Glacier do not tend to travel that far.
The ice sheet is up to three miles thick in its maximum. It is not static -- due to the
ongoing glaciation
, some of its
streams
advance southwards as fast as half a mile per year.
According to research conducted independently by both
Avallac'h
and
Nimue verch Wledyr ap Gwyn
, the entire civilization of the northern hemisphere is eventually doomed to extinction, covered beneath the spreading glacier over 3,000 years in the future.
The following is based on
The Witcher Thursdays
and may contradict
Andrzej Sapkowski
's works or R. Talsorian Games' official
The Witcher Role-Playing Game
line.
Brown Vale is primarily a frigid wasteland, only sparsely dotted with occasional
nunataks
and
crevasses
within the ice. Even the
ice giants
fear to venture into the heart of the glacier, preferring to keep solid ground beneath their feet; the undisputed rulers here are the
white dragons
, whose frost breath makes the heaviest snows of
Yamurlak
seem like a warm blanket in comparison. They hunt animals and snow
monsters
on the glacier and in the surrounding mountains and the tundram yet they do not hesitate to prey upon the supplies stockpiled by the Mayo Howler (or upon the Mayo Howler themselves).
Underneath this snowy dome, one can find a web of
glacier caves
that surround heat-giving geysers and volcanoes,
water reservoirs
with a thriving biosphere, and deposits of rare metals and gemstones rivaling in size those of
Kovir and Poviss
.
End of
The Witcher Thursdays
content.
