Munoz Hold
is located in the
Baker March
. This is where the alchemist takes Geralt to verify the incense formula that the witcher gives him.
Geralt can reach the laboratory in one of two ways: either by being led by Pugg while blindfolded, or by convincing Pugg to indicate the location on the witcher's map.
Inside the cave, there are a few items of interest: the
Formula: Tawny owl
and a
superb short sword
are the most interesting, but there are also some random crafting components.
There are also two mutually exclusive ways to leave the laboratory. Geralt can either take the boat back to
Flotsam
, or he can leave by the cave entrance, in which case the boat option disappears.
