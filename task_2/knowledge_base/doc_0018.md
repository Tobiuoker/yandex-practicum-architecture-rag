Anderson Stalker
was a member of the
House of Thyssen
. He was the son of
Godamba Thaess'en
and fathered an untold number of children, but the only name to survive was that of his eldest son,
Otto
.
He stylized himself as "Baron" though he was little more than a cruel warrior and robber that led a wasteful lifestyle and was later killed under unknown circumstances (possibly by his people when dividing plunder).
