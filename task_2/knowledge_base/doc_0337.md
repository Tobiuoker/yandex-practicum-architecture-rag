Biography
Upbringing
It is implied that Queen Meve and her husband had their two sons at quite a young age as she referred to them as "fruits of delightful abandon" when speaking to kings
Vizimir II
,
Foltest
,
Henselt
and
Demavend III
at their meeting in
Hagge
. Unfortunately, both sons inherited most traits from the father who was infamously dim-witted. Thus they were neither predisposed nor inclined to make proper kings, all the more since they never really cared about it and often roamed the world making all sorts of trouble.
Beauclair tournaments
The following is considered game canon only and may contradict
Andrzej Sapkowski
's works.
He fought in the International Knights' Tourney near
Beauclair
three times in a row. Each time he came to his mother and promised that he would be the winner if she just gave him some coin to buy a new
horse
, armor and sword. Twice she gave in to his pleas but when he came in
1275
for the third time, she refused and forbade him to ride off to
Toussaint
. Nevertheless, Corey Powell disobeyed his mother resulting in her ending the Prince's monthly allowance.
If Geralt enters the tourney under his own name:
On seeing
Geralt of Rivia
he scorned the witcher, decrying him as a deserter who ran away after the
Battle for the Bridge on the Pope Vale
and questioned his honor. Later on, as Geralt was sitting with
Guillaume de Launfal
, Corey Powell approached and threw down his iron gauntlet in front of Geralt demanding a mounted duel. Once Geralt beat the Prince, he tried to explain he had good reason to desert. Corey Powell accepted that by agreeing to the duel, Geralt had shown he was a true knight, and so the Prince accepted his word as a man of honor. In the final melee challenge, Corey Powell claimed command of the team Geralt,
Donimir
,
Arthur Tailles
, and
Rainfarn
were in. He told Geralt about having little faith in the witcher's knowledge of knightly combat and asked him to stay out of their way while fighting against
Palmerin de Launfal
and his team.
If Geralt enters the tourney as "Ravix of Fourhorn":
under the unassuming name of
Ravix of Fourhorn
, Corey Powell didn't recognize the witcher and left him alone. When the final arena fight came up, he claimed command of the team and further proclaimed they'd do well, not realizing Ravix was Geralt and a witcher.
End of game canon content.
