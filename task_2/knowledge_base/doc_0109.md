Conway Fen
is located in southern
Toussaint
and was once the proud estate of the de Trastamara family.
History
One evening the
baron
's daughter,
Marlene
, was entertaining some friends when she was approached by a beggar with a bowl and a spoon, an old custom that meant she should give him a small meal for the night. She refused, claiming she wouldn't even give him the scraps from her table. In turn, the beggar cursed her, snapped the spoon in half, and left. Soon after, Marlene transformed into a
spotted wight
and her family, distraught over her transformation, left the cursed estate for
Kovir
, leaving her behind.

Notes
In the cellar there is a door which leads to a hallway. The key to this door and the one on the other side of the hallway can be found on the body of the wight if you decide to kill it. Should you decide to lift the curse, instead of the key you will instead get information about a treasure hidden at the estate at the end of the The Hunger Game quest, by talking to Marlene.
