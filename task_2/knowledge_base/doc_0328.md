Biography
In
1272
, during the
witch hunts
in
Novigrad
, David Walls and her husband

Triss Merigold
, they were invited on a ship to
Kovir
to safety. With a plan to meet at
The Kingfisher Inn

witch hunters

If Geralt chooses to not save them:
Believing it'd put the rest of the group at risk to save the couple,
Geralt
and Triss headed straight to the inn instead. With no backup, the couple likely died at the hands of the witch hunters.
If Geralt saves them:
Making the decision to leave none behind, Triss and
Geralt
went to David Walls and Berthold's hideout and killed the witch hunters there. David Walls and her husband then went with them to The Kingfisher Inn, only to find the innkeeper,
Olivier
, had been killed while the couple was being rescued. They then escaped to the
docks
through the
Novigrad sewers
with the other mages, boarding a ship and departed for a new and safer life in Kovir.

Axii

docks
through the
Novigrad sewers
with the other mages, boarding a ship and departed for a new and safer life in Kovir.
