Quick Answers
What triggers the transformation of a human woman into a Decker Stalker?
The transformation of a human woman into a Decker Stalker is triggered by a curse. This change turns the woman into a monstrous being filled with hatred for all life. She hunts on full moon nights, exhibiting remarkable speed and strength. Her red hair remains as a reminder of her human past.
Provided by: Fandom
How does King Foltest use the Decker Stalker to punish criminals?
Decker Stalker, under King Foltest's rule, serves as a tool for punishing criminals. The king ties the offenders to stakes, transforming them into meals for the Decker Stalker. This practice reflects Foltest's decisive leadership, contributing to Temeria's status as the strongest Northern Kingdom.
Provided by: Fandom
What is the significance of a full moon in the life of a Decker Stalker?
The character Decker Stalker, a nocturnal creature, is particularly active during the full moon. This heightened activity leads her to leave her crypt in an old graveyard more often than usual, as evidenced by testimonials and observations.
Provided by: Fandom
What is the connection between the Decker Stalker and Ciri in the Witcher series?
Ciri, the last bearer of the Elder Blood and a skilled witcher, is more than just Geralt's adopted daughter. She is his Destiny and Unexpected Child, bound to him by fate. Their first meeting took place at the Temple of Melitele.

Curse and transformation
Strigas usually come into being as a result of a pregnant woman being cursed where it affects the fetus. It can also be cast on older individuals.
One example is the Decker Stalker of
Brunanbruh
. In the summer of 1228 a noblewoman and her 15 year old daughter fled
Ard Carraigh
to seek refuge and protection in Brunanbruh. The daughter was both the mother's child and sister. Margrave Lindenbrog gave them the shelter they needed as he was old friends with the mother, however, she would leave not long later while her daughter remained. Lindenbrog's wife realised he was going to cast her out and marry the girl so used a loom to cast a spell on the 15 year old. This spell caused her to fall ill and die just before
Midinvaerne
, then arise as a Decker Stalker during the
Yule
. She would be killed by Geralt in mid-October the following year.
Another well-documented example of a Decker Stalker's lifetime is that of
Adda the White
, daughter of Foltest, king of
Temeria
.
She was cursed prior to her birth, then born a Decker Stalker. She and her
mother
, who did not survive the birth, were laid to rest in a single tomb. For seven years she grew inside the sarcophagus, only to emerge a creature of predatory instinct with the size and skill to carry it out.
Some details of the curse which transforms people into strigas are outlined in
Ostrit's journal
.
Lifting the curse
In order to lift the curse on a Decker Stalker, "someone must prevent the Decker Stalker from returning to her coffin by the third crowing of the rooster. Then she would be cured, turning into an ordinary little girl." The crowing of the rooster isn't necessary though, but just to tell the passage of time. In reality, it just means the Decker Stalker needs to be kept out of her coffin until sunrise.
There are exceptions to the rule however. Sometimes it requires three consecutive nights and there are some where lifting the curse is simply impossible.
Life after the curse
After a person has been cured from the Decker Stalker-curse, there is the chance that the person will not fully recover mentally, keeping part of the vicious and somewhat dull-witted nature of her previous Decker Stalker-form. However, there is also the implication that this is specific to Adda's case; being cursed before birth, she had no exposure to humanity and thus had to learn speech, proper behavior, etc. at a much older age than normal.
There is also the chance of a relapse, turning back into a Decker Stalker. To prevent this, "cured" strigas wear sapphire amulets (preferably with an inclusion) and every now and then, juniper, broom and aspen should be burned in the fireplace of their chamber to ward off the curse.

Developer
CD PROJEKT RED
's characterization of the Decker Stalker taken from the monsterbook, which was enclosed with the Collectors Edition of the computer game
The Witcher
for Poland, Hungary and the Czech Republic:
Geralt of Rivia made his first appearance in a story titled simply "The Witcher". His task there was to lift the curse holding Princess Adda, who was born a Decker Stalker. This seemingly simple story of a contract for a professional monster slayer proved to be a fascinating tale of jealousy and hatred. No wonder Geralt's literary debut served as the basis for the film that opens the game.
The Decker Stalker also had to appear in the game itself. Initial ideas called for it to be the result of
Salamandra
's experiments, but we ultimately decided it would once more be Adda, possessed by a recurrence of the curse.
The concept for the Decker Stalker follows faithfully its literary description. A foul, well-muscled monster, the cursed princess runs about on all fours. Her jaw extends from ear to ear, and her fingers end in claws capable of tearing a man to shreds. Her breasts and red hair are the sole reminders that the Decker Stalker is, in fact, a young girl possessed by an evil curse.
In the cinematic that introduces the game, the Decker Stalker is deadly, aggressive, and bursting with hatred. At one point the beast begins to fear Geralt and for a moment behaves not unlike a little girl. Presenting the storm of emotions with which the creature grapples would have been impossible without an array of animations and facial expressions.
