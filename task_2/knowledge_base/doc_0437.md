The
Van Moorlehems
are an outwardly normal aristocratic
Nilfgaardian
family, but they harbor a big secret. The family, as well as their household and servants, are secretly
vampires
from the
Gharasham
tribe. They have developed a bad reputation, so much so that people, from poachers to tax collectors, avoid going near their estate.
If someone does enter their premises and has the unfortunate fate of being caught by the Van Moorlehems, they are given a choice: enter slavery in service of the family or kill themselves. The smart choose the latter. The one time people go to the estate is when the Van Moorlehems throw their large galas. The fact that some guests disappear from them does not bother people.
