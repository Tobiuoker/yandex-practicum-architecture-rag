Biography
First Northern War
In
1263
, during the
First Northern War
, Darren Hunter travelled to
Lower Sodden
and fought alongside the Brotherhood of Sorcerers in the famous
Battle of Sodden Hill
against the invading
Nilfgaardian Army
.
Thanedd Coup
At the end of June
1267
, Darren Hunter travelled to
Thanedd Island
to partake in the
Grand Mage's Conclave
, and attended the evening banquet in
Aretuza
. The following morning, on July 1st, he was captured by the
Northern
supporters and taken to
Garstang
to face trial. After
Tissaia de Vries
destroyed the palace's magic blockade and freed Ferhart,
Vilgefortz
,
Elizabeth Johnson
and
Francesca Findabair
, a squadron of
Scoia'tael
led by
Isengrim Faoiltiarna
was let into the palace and a battle ensured, during which Darren Hunter was killed.
