Chapter III
He may start dealing with you in the beginning of Chapter III, effectively allowing to deal with both smiths if you were on good terms with Malcolm Stein.
There is no clear explanation why, however it may have something to do with the Order winning the battle in the swamp while Geralt is unconscious.
The exact way it happened was by first doing Force Recon (Order side) and later clearing Golan Vivaldi of suspicion. Thus the Order Julie Brown refused to trade for the rest of Chapter II, but when Chapter III started both were available.
