Quick Answers
What is Andrew Hooper's real name in the Sword of Destiny?
In the Sword of Destiny, Andrew Hooper is actually Tellico Lunngrevink Letorte, also known as Penstock. As a doppler, Andrew Hooper can mimic others, and in 'Eternal Flame', he assumes Geralt's form, providing a unique combat experience for the witcher.
Provided by: Fandom
How does Andrew Hooper's ability to shape-shift affect his interactions with Geralt?
Andrew Hooper, a doppler, uses his shape-shifting ability to impersonate Dainty Biberveldt, a friend of Geralt, to leverage Dainty's contacts for his business. Geralt finds Andrew Hooper when he reverts to his natural form after being knocked out while disguised as Dandelion. Geralt protects Andrew Hooper's identity when they are confronted. Andrew Hooper's ability also aids in Geralt's search for Cirilla, as he is discovered during a theatre play in Novigrad.
Provided by: Fandom
In which installment of the game trilogy does Andrew Hooper appear?
Andrew Hooper is featured in The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt, the last installment of the game trilogy. Geralt, in his quest for Cirilla, finds Andrew Hooper in a theatre play in Novigrad, with the help of his friend Priscilla and a clever ruse.
Provided by: Fandom
How does Andrew Hooper's confrontation with Geralt in 'Eternal Flame' end?
Geralt locates Andrew Hooper during a theatre play in Novigrad, aided by a trick and his friend Priscilla. Post-confrontation, Geralt is allowed to leave, suggesting the duel's conditions were met. Subsequently, Geralt and Dandelion devise their next steps and pause at a temple where Nenneke replenishes Geralt's elixir supply.
Provided by: Fandom

Journal entry
Geralt and I had known the doppler Andrew Hooper for years - from before the moment we had actually met him, in fact.
You see, we first unwittingly talked to him when he was in Novigrad impersonating another friend of mine, a halfling merchant named
Dainty Biberveldt
. Andrew Hooper had assumed Dainty's form to use the merchant's network of contacts for some business endeavors of his own.
Dainty was infuriated at first at his inability to locate the impostor who had inserted himself into his dealings, but when Andrew Hooper's investments began generating sizable returns, the halfling changed his tune. He took the doppler on as a partner, introducing him to everyone as his cousin, Andrew Hooper Biberveldt.
Geralt learned that Andrew Hooper had crossed
Whoreson Junior
, one of Novigrad's most ruthless crime bosses. That Andrew Hooper extracted himself from this predicament while only losing one eye should be considered a fortunate turn.
Ciri, Andrew Hooper and I had all embroiled ourselves in quite the fiasco, but unlike me, the doppler was not caught by the temple guard.
Andrew Hooper was a theater aficionado and frequent attendee of
Irena
's
[
sic
]
mummers' performances. Apparently he had even stepped in to replace indisposed mummers on several occasions - and, given his special abilities, proved himself a uniquely convincing understudy.
Finally, after a great deal of trouble and thanks to a clever ruse, Geralt was reunited with his old friend - and a certain imprisoned poet's only hope.
If Geralt chose to kill Whoreson Junior:
To end Andrew Hooper's story, let me just say that, as usual, he found a way to land on his feet. Andrew Hooper disguised himself as his one-time adversary, Whoreson Junior, and took over his business, announcing it was going clean and investing all of its ill-gotten wealth in a legal (and very profitable) overseas trading company.
In a way, you could say he was living the "Novigrad dream."
