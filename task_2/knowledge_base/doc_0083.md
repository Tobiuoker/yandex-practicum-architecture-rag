The Hairy Bear Inn is located in the southernmost corner of the slums in the
Temple Quarter
. It is just kitty corner from the
Eager Thighs brothel
, and if you lack the orens for a proper room, there is a conveniently located campfire just outside.
The Hairy Bear is the place for indiscriminate clientèle. The innkeeper obviously holds back on the lye, though he waters down the ale generously enough. Patrons share the thin mattresses with rats, but most everyone can find their entertainment of choice here. Illegal
fistfight enthusiasts
, hustlers and drunkards will all find something to do at the Hairy Bear.
It is said that you can even buy the illegal drug
fisstech
here, provided you can find a dealer named
Coleman
.
