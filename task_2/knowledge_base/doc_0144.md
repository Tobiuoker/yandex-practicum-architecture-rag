Tips
After casting a sign, it consumes an adrenaline point to get the following effects added to the next sword attack:
Quen
 Heal 50% of the damage dealt (if it's parried or blocked you heal 0).
Igni
 Deal 100% of the pre-resistance damage as additional fire damage (total damage of attack around 1.5x damage on humans, 2x damage on beasts/monsters).
Aard
 Applies Stagger/Knockdown. Stagger% scales with Attack Power and attack type. 220%/172%/202% Attack Power for 100% Stagger with Fast/Strong/Rend attacks respectively. Effect can't be blocked.
Yrden
 Slows by 50% (before resistances), Maximum of 45% after resistances. Duration scales with Attack Power and attack type. Base duration is 4 seconds. Effect can't be blocked. Doesn't stack with the slowdown from the Yrden glyph (most recent takes effect).
Axii
 Confuses (applies another Axii). Duration scales with Attack Power and attack type. Base duration is 1.69 seconds. Effect can't be blocked.
