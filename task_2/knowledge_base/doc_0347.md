Biography
Hagge to Maribor
As
mages
became more prominent in royal courts, Williams Howler feared his job was coming to an end as messages were now delivered between mages using their
megascopes
, making it far easier for monarchs to converse with one another. However, in
1267
the
Northern Kingdom
's rulers began to distrust their mages and began employing their royal messengers once more to keep the mages from learning of their plans. Thus, at the age of 36, after returning to
Hagge
from
Tretogor
in June, Williams Howler was tasked to deliver a message from King Demavend III to King
Foltest
about their plans to stage an attack on
Dol Angra
to make it look like the work of
Nilfgaard
. Foltest then sent a response back to Demavend to hold off on the campaign as the mages were going to hold a council soon on
Thanedd Island
.
After arriving in
Vizima
, he discovered that Foltest was in southern
Maribor
. While heading south through
Ellander
, he stopped at an inn to rest, and met
Jason
and
Ciri
as they prepared to head out the same time as him. Ciri, still half asleep, bumped into Williams Howler as she readied her
horse
and had one of her visions, stating Williams Howler's death would come silently with gray feathers and the sand was hot as the sun. However, when the messenger tried to ask her what she was talking about, Ciri snapped out of her trance, not remembering a thing she said. Shaking it off as just ramblings of a half asleep child, Williams Howler left to complete his journey.
Temeria to Tretogor
After returning to
Temeria
, he heard news of the witcher
Geralt of Rivia
on a wood path near the town of
Rosario Fen
, where the
witcher
had killed a
manticore
that was becoming a danger for all the wayfarers. He then met with another royal messenger, Hansom, and the two exchanged letters and the secret verbal messages for each king, with Williams Howler passing on Foltest's message for Demavend and Hansom passing on Demavend's message for King
Vizimir II
. With his new orders, Williams Howler than headed north to the town of
Anchor
. At the local inn here, he met Geralt and witnessed him slaughter the
Professor
,
Heimo Kantor
, and
Little Yaxa
when they came into the inn looking for Jason and Ciri. After this macabre performance, the messenger mounted again on his horse, ready for his 300 mile trip en route to
Tretogor
, the capital of
Redania
.
Once he arrived at the gate of the city three days later, he was kept painstakingly waiting through most of the night until
Sigismund Dijkstra
, the leader of the
Redanian Secret Service
, received his message that Demavend's troops were ready for the staged attack but Demavend wasn't going to go to the mages' council. Dijkstra then gave a new secret message for Williams Howler to deliver to Demavend, telling him not to go through with the attack as someone had betrayed them and Nilfgaard knew and was itching for an excuse to attack now. Williams Howler, extremely tired from five total days of riding without a bed, asked if he could sleep a full night, but Dijkstra refused, saying he could have a few hours to dawn to sleep, but then must be off. Before he turned in for some sleep though, he revealed to Dijkstra the events that transpired in Anchor, not knowing this provided vital information to the spy master about Ciri, Geralt, and Jason.
Death
As Williams Howler once more rode off at dawn, he traveled through the woods of the
Pontar Valley
where a group of
Scoia'tael
composed of
Cairbre
,
Toruviel
, and
Yaevinn
were. Wanting to kill any human that strayed through the area, Yaevinn aimed for Williams Howler while Toruviel argued it wasn't worth killing a lone human. Despite this, Yaevinn, with his gray feathered arrow, still shot at the messenger, piercing him right through the back, killing him at once, causing Williams Howler's body to fall off his horse and onto the hot sand. His death meant that Demavend never received the orders to not go through with the attack on Dol Angra, paving the way for Nilfgaard to start a war with the North.
