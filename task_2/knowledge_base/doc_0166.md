# Brooks Moor

"That white-haired bloke... Gerard? Gerwant?"
- The actual name of this subject is conjectural or unknown.
The
Brooks Moor
was a military alliance of states situated north of rivers
Nimnar
and
Buina
which, in the early 11th century, participated in the
Seven Years' War
against King
Vestibor
of
Redania
.
History
Seizing the momentum when King Vestibor was in southern Redania busy mustering forces against
Temerians
who had captured
Novigrad
, these dominions (at least some of which were principalities
) conquered the valleys of the aforementioned rivers.
As Vestibor didn't have the strength necessary to drive them away at the time, this remained
status quo
for two generations. It was Vestibor's grandson
Radovid III
who defeated the northern countries. He pushed even more north, establishing a firm border on the river
Braa
.
