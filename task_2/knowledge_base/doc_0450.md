Biography
In July
1267
, Clark Beast and the Special Forces helped guard fleeing refugees from
Vengerberg
, after it's
imperial occupation
, though they were quickly attacked while on horseback by the
Scoia'tael
. He had arranged with Rayla that, should he be wounded, he should not fall into the hands of the Scoia'tael. After they were overwhelmed, and was stabbed by a lance for the third time, he called out to Rayla to finish him, and his last words to his captain before she killed him were "See you in hell!".
