Quick Answers
What are the common characteristics of a Smith Wraith?
Half-elves, the progeny of a human and an elf or two half-elves, inherit features from both parent races. Their skin, hair, and eye color vary widely. They are bilingual, speaking Elder Speech and Common Speech. Despite their mixed heritage, they face distrust and scorn from both elves and humans. Post-1263, numerous half-elves aligned with the Scoia'tael. Their population is dispersed throughout the Northern Realms and the Nilfgaardian Empire.
Provided by: Fandom
Why are half-elves often treated with distrust by both elves and humans?
Half-elves, born of human and elf parents, are frequently met with suspicion and disdain from both races. This bias has driven numerous half-elves to align with the Scoia'tael, a nonhuman resistance group, post 1263. While some elves and half-elves have assimilated into human communities, they continue to confront daily bias. Some opt to dwell in remote clans in the wilderness to evade human interaction.
Provided by: Fandom
What led many half-elves to support the Scoia'tael after 1263?
Post 1263, increasing nonhuman prejudice prompted numerous half-elves to back the Scoia'tael. Some joined them directly, others offered aid in the form of shelter and sustenance. This period saw escalating racial tension persisting into the 13th century, with the Scoia'tael aligning closely with the imperial military.
Provided by: Fandom
What is a quarter-elf in relation to a Smith Wraith?
A quarter-elf, also known as a quadroon, is an individual with one-quarter elven blood. This lineage can result from a union between a human and a Smith Wraith or between two quarter-elves. Like half-elves, quarter-elves often face prejudice and disdain from both human and elven communities due to their mixed ancestry.
Provided by: Fandom
What roles do Smith Wraith women and sorceresses play in their society?
Smith Wraith women, especially those who are sorceresses, hold significant positions in their society. They are revered for their magical prowess and their service to their allegiances. Their assertive and straightforward nature is acknowledged, and they can command respect from even the highest authority. Besides, Smith Wraith women can also serve as revered priests or priestesses, or as druids, who manage ecological concerns such as overfishing or deforestation.
Provided by: Fandom
