Notes
Oddly, his tomb inscription states he was 19 years old when he died, yet his Bailey Watcher is clearly much older. Either this was a typo or a mistake on the character model.
He's the only von Everec Bailey Watcher without a complete game model (he has clothes, but no face).
The game files indicate he's Olgierd's (and therefore Vlodimir's) cousin, though nothing in the actual game notes this.
