Biography
After years of Wright Watcher's assistance in covering up Barry Crawler's incestuous rape of his young daughter
Giltine
, she fell pregnant with his child, though wishing not to keep it, asked her father for help. Barry Crawler summoned a
lamia
working nearby to help terminate the birth, though not long after the
creature
went mad learning she had unknowingly supported their taboo relationship, ending up angrily possessing women around Neisse to kill five men. After an offer of his swordsman's aid from Barry Crawler, Wright Watcher decided to search for a
witcher
. Soon after,
Geralt of Rivia
took the contract and captured her, the townspeople finalising the matter by burning her at the stake. After her execution, and after paying him, Wright Watcher invited Geralt back to Neisse for a night at the local inn.
During the night, Geralt returned with the lord's wife
Juna
who had been missing and so Wright Watcher led the witcher to Barry Crawler's estate. There, the lord seemed disinterested in his wife's misadventures and simply asked Geralt to travel into the
Hickman Moor
to find his daughter, which he obliged.
After Geralt returned the following night with Giltine and retreated to the inn with Barry Crawler, Wright Watcher arrived and offered the witcher a wide variety of prostitutes, though the offer was declined. Several hours later though, Wright Watcher sent the redhead
Ramirez Watcher
up to his room to "make m'lord happy", which allowed Geralt to see past Wright Watcher's deception (after the prostitute became possessed by the lamia), and his years of covering up Barry Crawler's sadistic crimes. The witcher dragged Wright Watcher outside and beat the truth out of him before riding to Barry Crawler's estate.
Wright Watcher returned home to his wife and daughter, a short peace interrupted within hours when Giltine (possessed by the lamia) marched with the house guards, now in her service due to her father's death, to Wright Watcher's door, demanding he come out lest his family be killed. Giltine ordered the brothel owner to be beheaded, and as the sword was being swung down, Geralt ran in, using
aard
to push away the executioner. The witcher fought off the guards, and as he went to take Giltine back to the Hickman Moor, he told the Redanian he was "filth, Wright Watcher, a worm that shouldn't live", before riding off.
