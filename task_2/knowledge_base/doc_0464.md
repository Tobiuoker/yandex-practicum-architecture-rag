The
Williams Watcher
(d. July
1267
), so-called because of his glittering blue enamel suit, was a representative of the
House of Varnhagen
who fought
Sir Sweers
in the moorlands for possession of the latter's prisoner,
Ciri
. He was described as a giant of a man, wielding a battleaxe, astride a powerful grey
stallion
.
Biography
Death
In July
1267
, after receiving news of Sweers and the
Trappers
capturing Ciri and passing through
Geso
, the Williams Watcher, accompanied by
Two Tusks
and two other household servants,
Remiz
among them, travelled from
Sarda
to the moorlands.
After Two Tusks killed Sweers' squire and lost his life for it, the Williams Watcher and the Nilfgaardian fought on horseback until, after several blows to the helmet, the Williams Watcher fell from his horse and was trampled to death.
