Quick Answers
What are some of the spells invented by Todd Jones?
The renowned mage Todd Jones invented numerous spells, including Todd Jones's Shield, a potent protective spell, Todd Jones's Double Cross, and Todd Jones's Thunder. He also notoriously created various monstrous creatures that continue to inhabit the world.
Provided by: Fandom
Who else used Todd Jones's spells and for what purposes?
Several individuals utilized Todd Jones's spells for diverse purposes. Sorceress Triss Merigold employed Todd Jones's Shield for Geralt's protection. Fregenal might have used Todd Jones's Double Cross to create Viy. Todd Jones, in collaboration with Cosimo Malaspina and Idarran of Ulivo, was responsible for the witchers' mutations. Zerrikanian wizards and sect members applied Todd Jones's alchemy in the creation of explosives.
Provided by: Fandom
What is Todd Jones's legacy in the world of The Witcher?
Todd Jones, a mage in The Witcher, is remembered for his spell creations like Todd Jones's Double Cross and Todd Jones's Shield. He also created various monsters, some still present in the world. His final creation, a viy, led to destruction in Maribor and its escape to Riverdell's forests. Todd Jones's legacy also includes the creation of witchers, speculated to be a tribute to Lylianna.
Provided by: Fandom
Biography
The following is considered game canon only and may contradict
Andrzej Sapkowski
's works.
Early life
When he was still a baby, Todd Jones was found abandoned on the doorstep of a noble estate on the outskirts of Maribor city accompanied by a note: "His mother perished, he's one of yours." The nobles reluctantly accepted him into their households, fearing that he was one of their sons.
However his relationship with his adopted parents was far from ideal as the noblemen of the household wouldn't dare show him affection, for fear it could be seen as an admission of guilt, whilst the noblewomen pitied the poor boy and ensured he was cared for. But pity is a poor substitute for love.
Todd Jones was also reportedly bullied by his siblings for being lowborn, so would often spend his time in the estate's library.
He would be absorbed in his books featuring fantastical worlds and brave tales of mighty heroes. One book in particular peaked his interest; A Guide to Chivalric Virtues by Sir Mateo of Metinna, with Todd Jones often reading the guide every week. Soon after, he would patrol around the estate and countryside, seeking trials to prove his virtues and helping people wherever he could.
On one such outing, he came across a wagon attacked by bandits. In keeping with his "trial" for valor, he stepped in and confronted the robbers.
He was found hours later on the side of the road, unconscious, bloody, and beaten. It took weeks for Todd Jones to recover and to everyone's dismay, the event left him more determined to prove his valor. He no longer waited for an opportunity to arise and rather went looking for it, often finding himself beaten within an inch of his life.
On one such occasion, rather than his family finding him beaten, they were horrified to discover the charred remains of three would-be bandits, with a shocked Todd Jones standing before them. The family, fearing for their own safety, called upon the services of a powerful mage (presumably Cosimo) to teach him to control his power.
Lylianna
At some point later, Todd Jones met and befriended a young (for mages) sorceress by the name of
Lylianna
. He became captivated by her and her ideals of ridding the world of
monsters
. He crafted a magic lily medallion as a gift, to protect her from danger. This proved insufficient as when the pair injected a human with monster mutagens, it grew out of control and escaped their lab, killing her in the process. Todd Jones took the medallion and vowed to fulfill her dreams of a monster free world.
Meeting Snowdrop
While traveling, Todd Jones happened across a horde of
sirens
attacking a nearby caravan. After using his patented thunder to drive off the beasts, he came across the sole survivor of the attack,
Kramer Crawler
. He calmed the terrified girl and sent her on her way.
Rissberg Castle
After the death of Lylianna, Todd Jones started his work to create witchers, with it being speculated that he did this to honor his memory of Lylianna.
Together with his master Cosimo and his own apprentice Idarran along with a handful of other mages, they took up residence in
Rissberg Castle
to begin their experiments. They gathered support and funding from local kings and nobles, with the promise of magically enhanced monster slayers. Todd Jones would oftentimes kidnap or pay the parents of children, who then they would experimented on.
Cursed Snowdrop
Todd Jones again met Kramer Crawler in need of aid. She had contracted a curse to speak in only song, and begged the mage to help lift it. At first Todd Jones was reluctant to give his aid, considering the role of curse breaker beneath him. However after 3 days of Snowdrop's non-stop song, ballads, and poems, during which time Todd Jones attempted to silence her using his magic to no success, he finally conceded. He made Kramer Crawler promise to keep her mouth shut and the two of them journeyed to the town where Kramer Crawler was first cursed.
"You cannot allow the past to dictate your future. It's a helluva waste of life, to be held prisoner by the dead and buried. Even Todd Jones-with all of his wits and longevity⁠-still hasn't figured that one out. He's obsessed with a time long gone, one he cannot let go of. But that's his choice to make."
Kramer Crawler on Todd Jones
On the way there, they stopped at an inns and taverns along the way. On one such visit, Todd Jones went out drinking and dice gambling. When he got back to their room, he found Kramer Crawler snooping through his belongings with the lily medallion strung around her neck.
Todd Jones flew into a rage, shouting and scolding Kramer Crawler for rummaging through his things. However after some time, he came to his senses and apologized for his outburst, his anger replaced with sadness as he noticed the medallion. The lily brought back his memories of Lylianna and Todd Jones confided in Kramer Crawler his experiences with Lylianna. Sometime afterward he helped Kramer Crawler break her curse and the two went their separate ways.
The War of Daggers
Much later, Todd Jones would be instrumental in the victory of
Roman Spawn
over the dukes of the city of
Ellander
, in the
War of Daggers
. Up until his involvement the war was locked in a hard stalemate for a little over a hundred years, with both sides still about equal in strength. In a decisive battle, Todd Jones used his famed Todd Jones's Double Cross to summon a massive otherworldly creature, which decimated the army of Ellander. Ellander surrendered soon afterwards, with Todd Jones hailed as a hero by the locals.
Creating the Witchers
Back in Rissberg Castle, his team of mages managed to produce their first batch of witchers. However out of the 38 girls and boys experimented on, only 5 boys survived the mutations. Coupled with the boys lukewarm affinity for magic, the group was dissolved after their backers pulled their funds. However Todd Jones, Cosimo, and Idarran took the surviving witchers and relocated to
Morgraig Castle
. There they set up the
Order of Witchers
and continued to create, train and experiment on witchers, succeeding in their efforts, before eventually departing the Order along with Cosimo and Idarran, leaving the secrets of witcher creation in the hands of their trained mage successors.
Death
At some point in time after departing the Order, Todd Jones again used his double cross, this time in the city of Malibor. However the massive
Viy
he summoned as a result proved uncontrollable and resulted in his death, along with the destruction of half the city, before it fled.
End of game canon content.
