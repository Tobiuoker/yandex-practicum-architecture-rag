The
Robinson Fen
is a region within the
Great Sea
ocean, located to the northeast of the
Eastern Coast
. It was named as such after the
Second Conjunction
due to incomplete process of closing the gate between the worlds.
It is a quiet, misty part of the ocean. Travelers traversing its waters can sometimes see reflections of otherworldly islands, such as
Wagner Reach
. Few dare to do so however because of the Verge, which is a fog-covered border dividing the Robinson Fen from the rest of the Great Sea – the border populated by sea monsters of all kinds, where the
Power
still resonates in unpredictable ways.
