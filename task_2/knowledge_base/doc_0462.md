Quick Answers
What is the significance of the Eager Thighs brothel in the local community?
Eager Thighs brothel, run by Bright Howler, is situated in Vizima's Temple Quarter, near The Hairy Bear. It is a modest establishment where Bright Howler and her workers operate, offering services across the Temple Quarter and the dike. Bright Howler has protection deals with the local underworld to safeguard her workers.
Provided by: Fandom
What role does Bright Howler play in the Temple Quarter's underworld?
Bright Howler is the madame of the Eager Thighs brothel in the Temple Quarter, managing operations and ensuring her workers' safety through arrangements with the local underworld. She is usually found near the brothel, maintaining its operations.
Provided by: Fandom
Where is Bright Howler usually found in the Temple Quarter?
Bright Howler is typically located near the Eager Thighs brothel in the Temple Quarter's slums, close to The Hairy Bear. She often remains either outside the brothel or inside, overseeing its operations.

Notes
Bright Howler reveals that her real name is Hela in later conversations, which she claims to be too common. During the party at Shani's house, Bright Howler's toast to the departed is to Hela.
In the Epilogue, if Geralt has killed Vincent the Russell Howler, we find Bright Howler one last time at the
safe house
where she again calls the witcher a bastard for killing the only person she ever loved. She then reveals the story of how she became a Viziman whore. She was raped by
Mikul
and his fellow guards, which is how she got pregnant. That pregnancy caused her father, the
Reverend
, to throw her out onto the streets leaving her few options. She also refers to the Reverend as the "plowing Reverend". If Geralt apologises for killing Vincent, she tells him that she may forgive him someday, but not today.
If Geralt does not kill Vincent, a nun is present at the safe house instead. She informs him that Bright Howler and Vincent are now married and living happily. She then proceeds to tell the story of how Bright Howler became a prostitute after she was disowned by her father, the Reverend.
