Biography
The following is considered game canon only and may contradict
Andrzej Sapkowski
's works.
Born into a noble family, Reynard enlisted at the age of 20 and served under
King
Reginald
of
Rivia
. He was immediately granted the rank of lieutenant due to his status and so, wanting to prove his merit, Reynard began to criticize the king's poor decisions publicly. A superior officer reported Reynard and he was charged with lèse-majesté, the crime of offence against a reigning sovereign or state. At his trial, he was found guilty of treason and sentenced to hang for his crimes, however Reginald delayed the execution and asked he repeat every word Reynard had said against him, which he did. Reginald agreed with the offensive comments, and appointed Reynard his personal aide, seeing potential in the man.
Reynard participated in battles against Nilfgaard while holding the rank of captain; he was eventually appointed general.
In
1259
, when Reginald died, he started serving under his wife, Queen Meve. Unbeknownst to him, Reginald had told Meve he was the only one she could trust.
In
1267
, he traveled with Meve to
Hagge
for a strategic meeting of the Northern monarchs. On the return journey,
Nilfgaardian
troops under General
Ardal aep Dahy
invaded Lyria. He took part in the
Battle of Dravograd
, where Queen Meve defeated the invaders and saved the city of
Dravograd
.
When the queen however returned in the
capital
, she was victim of a
conspiracy
against her organized by Count
Caldwell
and by her own son
Villem
, who was placed on the throne and surrendered the country to Ardal aep Dahy. Both the queen and Reynard were imprisoned but fortunately they were freed by
Gascon
and the
Strays of Spalla
before dawn, when Count Caldwell planned to kill them both.
Together with a few of his own loyal men and Gascon, Reynard and the queen fled from the city and traveled through
Aedirn
recruiting soldiers for her
new army
in order to take her kingdom back. Seeing Meve's resentment towards her son and knowing Villem had been shielded from the worst aspects of the Empire, Reynard established contact with the prince, encouraging him to meet the queen in
Mahakam
and see her point of view. The meeting did not end in reconciliation as Reynard had hoped and mother and son parted ways once more.
In
Pearson March
, Reynard realised that Gascon had been hoping to trade Meve to the Nilfgaardians for amnesty and informed her; Gascon professed his loyalty and in turn, revealed Reynard's part in the meeting with Villem. As he had gone behind her back, Meve saw Reynard's actions as a betrayal. She was deeply hurt and her trust in him was not fully restored until he helped save her from the spy
Gwalter aep Llwynog
.

After the Battle for the Bridge, Reynard helped Meve in freeing
Rivia
from the Nilfgaardian occupation, until the queen's army finally arrived at the
City of Rivia
, where General Ardal aep Dahy himself placed his main headquarters. During a brief meeting with aep Dahy, Meve learned that Nilfgaardian reinforcements were coming and her army was trapped. However, Reynard proposed a solution - a small team could infiltrate the
castle
and open the gate, allowing the rest of the troops to
besiege
the city and take control before aep Dahy's reinforcements arrived. Since he knew the castle's layout best, Reynard volunteered himself. Gascon, who felt far more qualified in stealth, also volunteered, stating that Reynard was Meve's right hand and should be next to her in battle. It was ultimately left to Meve to decide who would undertake the mission.
If Reynard survives the battle:
Reynard fought alongside the queen to regain the city, and thus their country. With General aep Dahy's death at the
Second battle of Aldersberg
and later the
Peace of Cintra
, the war finally ended. As Meve resumed rule over Ward Moor, Reynard remained, as ever, by her side - though no longer only as a general and advisor, but also her partner and lover.
If Reynard dies in the battle:
Reynard was chosen to open the Rivia Castle gate. Although he accomplished his task, he and his soldiers were attacked and killed before Meve could reach them. His sacrifice was not in vain, as his city and thus his country were freed. After the war's end, Reynard was buried in the chapel of Rivia Castle, laid alongside the tombs of famous dukes and kings. Droves of his faithful soldiers bid him farewell, while Queen Meve herself brought flowers on his shrine each day.
Personality
As a career soldier, Reynard was a capable strategist and commander. He had an excellent memory, having been said to know the history of seven generations of noble families, and was able to translate both
Nilfgaardian
and the
Elder Speech
to some degree. However, he took his level of professionalism to a fault and was regarded by other members of Meve's retinue as stiff and overly serious with a "lance [up] his arse". He did not casually socialize with his subordinates as he believed it to be improper, but he did care for them and often spoke up for their interests.
He was Meve's closest confidante and provided her with guidance and support, though he was also unafraid of expressing his disapproval. In general he advocated for merciful and honorable solutions to her dilemmas even if they were risky. At some point he developed feelings for her, but kept them to himself and continued to treat her with total formality.
Still, his devotion to the queen - dutiful or otherwise - was readily apparent, shown by occasions such as her knighting of Geralt when he took greater offense to a perceived slight against her than she did.
Trivia
"Reynard" is a Germanic name meaning "strong in counsel".
