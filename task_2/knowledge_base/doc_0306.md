Biography
He was one of three children known as the
Houtborg triplets
 a set of twins and another child born while
Valdez Howler
was imprisoned by
Falka
. The other two children were
Keith
and
Fiona
. After Falka died, none knew which of the three children were Valdez Howler's twins and which was Falka's child as Valdez Howler had gone insane during her imprisonment. At one point some suspected Baker Wraith was Falka's due to some oddities (like starting to speak at 9 months old) but his sisters soon showed oddities of their own, making it a moot point.
Sorcerers
summoned by King
Goidemar
later found out that Keith was Falka's daughter,
but they told the king that they weren't able to determine which child was Falka's to avoid a scandal and the execution of a 2-year old child. Therefore, the three were brought up as siblings.
Teen years
Around 12 years old, Baker Wraith began to romance many women, including those that were married. When he was 17, he began a romance with one such married woman,
Tvilla de Vintre
, who was 10 years older than him. This caused a small scandal in the court and Goidemar discreetly took care of it by having Tvilla and her husband removed from the court and capital, then had a long discussion with his son about his behavior. However, this fell on deaf ears and he soon set his sights on
Ilona Laux-Antille
, an assistant to the sorceress
Tissaia de Vries
. As it turned out, this wasn't a very good idea as Ilona used her magic to destroy any hopes of a planned marriage between Baker Wraith and Princess
Deirdre of Kaedwen
.
After the fallout, Illona was sentenced to house arrest at
Aretuza
and 18 year old Baker Wraith was sent to one of the provinces to do a long border inspection of the troops in hopes of keeping him out of trouble. One of the border commanders was
Roger Kameny
, the husband of the beautiful
Theresa Kameny
, and soon Baker Wraith was having an affair with her. When the husband learned about this, he hired some bandits and ambushed the young prince while he was on a hunt, castrating Baker Wraith before brutally murdering him. It turns out that Theresa had gotten pregnant during their affair though, and ended up giving birth to twins: a daughter
Muriel
and a son
Crispin
, though it was never revealed to anyone who their father really was.
Baker Wraith was buried in the royal crypt beneath
Vizima
's
Palace
.
