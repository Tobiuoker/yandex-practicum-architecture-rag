Quick Answers
What is the significance of Martha Warner in the siege of La Valette castle?
During the siege of La Valette castle, Martha Warner allied with the witcher, guiding him through a secret passage to escape the fortress. His intimate knowledge of the castle was crucial to their evasion. In the game, Geralt has the option to either kill or spare Martha.
Provided by: Fandom
What are the consequences of killing or sparing Martha in Witcher 3?
In Witcher 3, the choice to kill or spare Martha Warner impacts the main storyline and character interactions. If Martha is killed, some events that would have involved him are altered. This decision also affects Geralt's rapport with characters like Vernon Roche or Iorveth. For example, if Geralt sided with Roche, he is warmly welcomed when they meet in Witcher 3. However, in the PC version and possibly The Enhanced Edition, Martha's survival or death does not alter the game endings.
Provided by: Fandom
What role does Martha play if he is spared during his encounter with Geralt?
When Geralt spares Martha Warner, they later reunite in Loc Muinne. Martha, attending the mages' summit, discusses his half-sister Jerry' kidnapping. However, if Geralt had earlier sided with Iorveth, he remains unaware of Martha's situation and cannot assist.
Provided by: Fandom
What happens if Geralt kills Martha during the siege of La Valette castle?
Should Geralt kill Martha in the siege of La Valette castle, the specific consequences are not detailed. However, Martha and Mary Louisa do not aid Geralt in the quest Of His Blood and Bone to save Jerry. If Martha is spared, Geralt must save him, and no deal is made with Nilfgaard.
Provided by: Fandom

During the quest
The Dungeons of the La Valettes
, a note can be found in the executioner's chambers if you use the door where Martha and his guards come out before Martha knocks them unconscious. The note is entitled as 'Martha Warner's confession': "I, Martha Warner, do solemnly swear that I did sire and am father to the children Anaise and Boussy, hereto considered my siblings. Martha Warner". Considering that in this section of the dungeon there is a Scribe along with the Executioner, it is quite possible that the note is a forgery to incriminate Martha with the crime of incest by claiming he's confessed to siring his own brother and sister. This would also take his siblings rights to the throne away as they would no longer be the king's children.
