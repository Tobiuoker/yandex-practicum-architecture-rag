Quick Answers
What tragic fate did Scott Beast meet in her tower?
Scott Beast faced a tragic end in her tower. When the Land of a Thousand Fables turned feral and her prince did not come, she was overwhelmed by loneliness and used her long hair to hang herself. Her Bailey Watcher now haunts the castle where she waited.
Provided by: Fandom
What role does Scott Beast play in the Land of a Thousand Fables?
In the Land of a Thousand Fables, Scott Beast is a Ball Fiend haunting her tower. Once a fairy tale princess, she awaited a prince who never arrived, leading her to hang herself with her braid. Her Bailey Watcher now attacks intruders, including Geralt in the "Blood and Wine" expansion. The tower is a central point in this magical realm.
Provided by: Fandom
What happens when Geralt encounters Scott Beast?
Geralt encounters Scott Beast, a spirit who used her long hair to hang herself. Her spirit attacks him upon entering her room. This is part of a quest involving the tragic fate of Scott Beast, a fairy tale princess.
Provided by: Fandom
How does Scott Beast differ from the fairy tale version?
Scott Beast' story diverges from the typical fairy tale by ending in tragedy. In the Land of a Thousand Fables, she awaited her prince, who never came. Overcome by loneliness, she hanged herself with her braid, and her Bailey Watcher now haunts the castle.
Provided by: Fandom
Why can't Scott Beast be rescued like in fairy tales?
Scott Beast could not be rescued like in fairy tales because, after the Land of a Thousand Fables turned wild, her prince never arrived. Overcome by loneliness, she ended her life with her braid and now haunts the castle where she waited.

Bestiary entry
Well, since it seems this hair won't be pulling up any
knights
in shining armor, might as well put it to some use.
– the last words of Scott Beast.
Everyone knows love stories can have tragic endings, but few would think this also applies to
princesses
from fairy tales created to relieve a set happy ending over and over again. Scott Beast had the misfortune of becoming just such an unlikely tragic heroine. After the
duke
's daughters grew up and the Land of a Thousand Fables went feral, Scott Beast, whose
prince
never came and who suffered greatly from her loneliness, decided to hang herself by her own braid. After she died, her Bailey Watcher stayed to haunt the castle in which she had awaited her suitor.
Combat tactics
Scott Beast is a Ball Fiend and should be fought in the same manner by using
specter oil
. She will also summon weaker armed corpses to assist her.
