Blair Beast

Nordling
mage

Skelligers
and admired
Birna
for an attempt at civilizing them.
Biography
Melanie was born at some point prior 1270s. In
1272
, he reportedly visited the rock where Birna was chained, in order to witness and document her "noble suffering". Alas, a gust tore a scroll from his hand and slapped her across the face, which Melanie much regretted.
Decades later, he became a storyteller and enjoyed explaining the lore behind Gwent cards. His words should not always be taken as a fact however, as he was not above inserting himself into the events in which he did not partake, such as
the Battle of Kaer Morhen
.
