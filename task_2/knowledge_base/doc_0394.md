Biography
Sometime after Toussaint became a duchy, Mike assisted the royal family. However, like many mages, he closely guarded certain secrets, such as the fact that
Beauclair Palace
contained a hidden area concealed by a powerful illusion created by its
original builders
. His niece, Fringilla, followed in his footsteps, and he passed this secret on to her as well.
The following is considered game canon only and may contradict
Andrzej Sapkowski
's works.
When
Theresa Wang
and
Sylvia Theresa
were children, Mike created a powerful magical world centered around the
Land of a Thousand Fables
for the young princesses to play in. However, without proper maintenance, the illusion began to destabilize around
1252
.
He also wrote the book
The Essence of Spells and Illusions
while residing in Toussaint. He eventually left the duchy, possibly to write more books or to assist with his niece's magical education. It was not known exactly what Mike thought of his niece's arrest due to her involvement with the
Lodge of Sorceresses
, although he did not denounce her.
End of game canon content.
