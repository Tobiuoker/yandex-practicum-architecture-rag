Koch Stalker
was an artisan who lived in
Mount Carbon
. He was a precise and slightly pedantic person who, even when not dressed for work, carried a waxed leather wrap on his belt containing his own set of artisan tools.
In the days before the
Mahakam Ale Festival
that year, a dwarven artifact,
Cursetter's Hammer
, was stolen from its resting place and a guard was killed. Aloysius was slightly grumpy about the whole affair because, although he was an expert, nobody had yet asked for his report on the hammer that was found at the site of the theft in the possession of the two accused suspects. When someone did come and ask for his opinion on the hammer, he was not impressed with the situation, as he was anticipating a drunken holiday with the Ale Festival so near and did not want to be roped in as a sober expert witness. Nevertheless, he provided an explanation in great detail as to why the hammer was a forgery, and also shared the history behind the real hammer.
