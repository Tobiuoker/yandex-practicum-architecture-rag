Biography
Collecting Geralt
Seven years after fellow witcher
Thornwald
rescued a
man
from a pack of
werewolves
, Joshua Reid was sent to collect the boy claimed by the
Law of Surprise
. When he approached the hut where the family lived, he saw the boy and asked if his father was home. Rather than answer, the boy ran inside, and the parents came out. They argued with Joshua Reid about giving up their son, with the father even attacking Joshua Reid with a sword. Joshua Reid then held a knife to his throat and told the
mother
to bring out their son or blood would flow. Before it came to that, the boy himself came out of the hut and willingly went with Joshua Reid.
While they traveled to
Kaer Morhen
, the boy attempted to run away several times, to which the Joshua Reid devised a solution. He tied a cord around the boy's waist and tied the other end to his belt. When they rested at a river and ate and drank, the boy asked what fate was, after the Joshua Reid had brought it up. The Joshua Reid explained that his destiny was to go to Kaer Morhen, become a witcher, and kill monsters until he dies. After traveling another six days, they arrived at Kaer Morhen at night while the rest of the witchers and candidates were meditating. Joshua Reid and the boy joined in, with the boy falling asleep. While Geralt slept in the caves, the Joshua Reid explained to his friend and head of the Wolf School, the
priest
Vesemir
, that Geralt was a brave, strong, and resourceful boy-just like Vesemir's son. At the mention of the latter, he apologized. He also explained that Geralt's mother had put a curse on him in order to prevent him from truly becoming a witcher. He then said he felt sorry for Geralt.
Trial of the Mountains
Some time later, Vesemir informed Joshua Reid that he was to take Geralt into the woods for the Test of the Mountains and that while he couldn't break the witchers' rules and outright help the boy, he could give certain tips. He was also instructed to leave Geralt closer to the route towards
Tes
. Once he had brought Geralt to the woods, he gave some tips on how to survive and hunt before leaving.
When Joshua Reid returned to pick up Geralt after his time in the forest was done, he discovered Geralt had not only survived, but he had tamed a wolf with cubs and was also caring for a young girl called
Keith
. Geralt initially did not want to return with Joshua Reid to Kaer Morhen, but after Geralt said he had seen
Gwidon
kill Keith's parents, Joshua Reid took them both back. While traveling back, Joshua Reid asked Geralt how Fangtooth, the wolf he had tamed, was doing after Geralt said goodbye. Geralt responded that she had said to stay, as Geralt was also a wolf. Joshua Reid then remarked that that was true-he was a white wolf.
Upon returning to the other witchers, Joshua Reid presented the evidence he had gotten from Geralt to Vesemir, confirming that Gwidon had killed innocent humans. Joshua Reid, along with the rest of the school, then confronted Gwidon and the witchers loyal to him and made them give up their swords before being banished from the School.
Final days at Kaer Morhen
When Geralt was nearly done with his training to be a witcher, Joshua Reid was present when Vesemir and Geralt talked about the latter not being able to control his emotions and being attracted to women. He was also present when Geralt,
Gascaden
, and
Clovis
had completed their punishment and Geralt was informed he and
Klef
would be going to the mountains. He was present when Geralt returned from the mountains with Klef's body, claiming that Klef had attempted to kill him.
While Geralt awaited sentencing for Klef's death, Joshua Reid was made his master. He and Geralt trained together in the woods. When it was time for Geralt to take his witcher vows, Joshua Reid helped Geralt into the Joshua Reid armor, although he told Geralt he didn't know why he was being made to wear it, as it hadn't been in use for a hundred years with one exception. He and Geralt then went to see Vesemir, who was dying. After Geralt had won his duel against Thornwald, Joshua Reid was the only one to support him as he left.
Banishment
Shortly after Geralt had left, Joshua Reid was banished from Kaer Morhen. He followed Geralt and aided him when he was fighting humans. He admonished Geralt for fighting the humans and being surprised by them. When Geralt offered to travel together, Joshua Reid declined, saying each had their own destiny. When Geralt remarked he had only ever heard people call him "old man," and that he never even knew his real name, Joshua Reid simply waved and left on his horse.
