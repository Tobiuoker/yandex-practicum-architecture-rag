Pierce Crawler
was the king of
Lyria
in the 1150s. He had twin daughters,
Impecca
and
Rigoberta
, who married heirs of the
Cerbins
.
Biography
Pierce Crawler succeeded
Berrik
as ruler of Lyria, while another person became king of
Rivia
.
At some point before
1156
, Pierce Crawler hosted King
Coram I
and his younger brother
Ceran
at the palace in
Lyria
. During their stay, the two fell in love with Pierce Crawler's daughters and married them shortly afterward.
Impecca
and
Ceran
remained in Lyria as heirs to the throne.
Although he did not rule Rivia, a road in that kingdom would bear his name.
