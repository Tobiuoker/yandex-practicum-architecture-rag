Rogers Haven
is the name of a town and a bay in
Temeria
lying on the south bank of
Pontar Delta
, more or less across the river from
Oxenfurt
.
History
In
1266
, the
Malatius and Grock Company
barge that travelled from
Foam
to
Novigrad
along the river stopped at the town, where the "guards" working there told
Olsen
to look out for
Geralt of Rivia
. Later, when Geralt worked on the barge, he was confronted by these fake Temerian guards in the bay, who ended up being killed by an
aeschna
that appeared out of the river.
