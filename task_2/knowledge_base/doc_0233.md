Biography
Early Life
Adela was traveling with her mother and several other families. One day, they were approached by a group of witchers under the command of
Gwidon
. The group surrounded the caravan and, upon Gwidon's signal, killed all the adults and abducted the children. During the attack, Adela was wounded.
She was found by Geralt, who had witnessed the attack during his trial in the forest. He nursed her back to health. When she regained consciousness, she recognized the necklace Geralt was wearing as her mother's. Geralt commented that he had found it on the road. Realizing what that meant, Geralt comforted her and told her to be brave.
When the
Old Witcher
came to pick up Geralt after the trial's end, the children told him what had happened. They were then brought to
Kaer Morhen
, where they testified to the witcher council about the attack and were present when Gwidon and his group were banished.
Life as Witcher
Unlike Geralt, Adela did not remain at the Wolf School. She went to a witcher school beyond the mountains, where she underwent training.
One day, she returned to Kaer Morhen with two of her fellow witchers. On their way, they came across Geralt,
Clovis
, and
Gascaden
, who were nearly fighting after Gascaden had made a remark to Clovis. Upon approaching them, Adela greeted the group and remarked that they were drinking potions without an instructor present, warning them to be careful.
As she began to leave, Geralt asked for her name and said he had seen her before, to which Adela replied that he had not.
Sometime later, she was swimming in a lake when Geralt approached her. She asked what the matter was and if he recognized her, as he had claimed before. When he said he was mistaken, she agreed, asking how he could possibly know women. She then said she would now watch as he swam.
When Geralt began undressing, she asked if he was truly a witcher, since witchers were supposed to be asexual-something he clearly was not. When he replied that he didn't know, she grabbed her things and left.
A training duel was later organized between Adela and her fellow witchers against Geralt and Clovis. Adela chose to duel Geralt. During the duel, Geralt mostly used evasive maneuvers and even disarmed her, earning a reprimand from
Roger
. When Geralt re-engaged in the duel, he let Adela win.
She was present when Geralt, Gascaden, and Clovis began their punishment, and also when the witcher council informed Geralt that he had been found not guilty of the charges brought against him.
While Geralt was training in the woods under the watch of the Old Witcher, Adela sought him out and asked if he wanted to train with her, as she was also leaving on a mission-but Geralt couldn't.
She approached Geralt again, this time while he was alone. She measured his pulse, thinking he was in a trance, and was surprised by how high his heartbeat was. Concluding she had surprised him, she asked if he had been thinking about her.
Geralt asked how she knew and when she would be leaving. As she was departing soon, he asked if she could teach him how to control his heartbeat. She had him place his hand on her chest to feel her own heartbeat.
They met up again later and were intimate. Afterward, she informed Geralt she was leaving the next day. When Geralt asked her to stay, she became upset, saying they would never meet again and that he wasn't a real witcher.
As he tried to approach her, she drew her sword and told him to leave, calling him a nobody. As he left, she thrust her sword into the ground in frustration.
She then reported to Roger but said nothing-only nodded. When Roger asked if she would testify before the council and if Geralt was a man, she shook her head. This made Roger angry, and he told her to leave. She then rode off from Kaer Morhen, smiling one last time at Geralt.
