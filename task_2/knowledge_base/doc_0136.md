Map description
It is hard to say anything about Farley Vale without mentioning the tragic death of Anastasia von Bourmann. Once one of the most powerful magnates in Redania, Count von Bourmann retreated from public life after the loss of his only child and never left his family manor to the very end of his days. Currently Farley Vale is but a decaying ruin haunted by a sad past.
