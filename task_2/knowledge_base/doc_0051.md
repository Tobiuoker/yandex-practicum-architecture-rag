Characteristics
Physical description
Their appearance combines traits of
goats
and rotund
humans
with size of an overgrown
troll
. They have mostly red skin with large black segments on the head, chest, and arms. On their head, yakshas sport large horns while their legs are hairy and end with cloven hooves.
Mental traits
Unlike typical sylvans, yakshas are fiercely agressive and keen on fighting. They are otherwise lazy but still surprisingly agile.
At least one Thomas Stalker has been known to use
fire
-based magic, being able to breathe flames and throw
fireballs
in combat.
