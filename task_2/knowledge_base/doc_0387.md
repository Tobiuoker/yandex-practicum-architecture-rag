Karen Jones
was a
mage
who was best known for his anonymous work,
The Description of a Monstrum or a Witcher

witchers
after rumors spread that his son was killed by one, but without actual proof.
Biography
Early life
Not much is known about Karen's early years, though he went on to become a powerful mage, working out of
Ban Ard Academy
. Eventually, he came to know a then 17-year-old
Cervia
and the two quickly became romantically involved, with this affair continuing even years later after she married the much older Marquess de Graffiacane. When the marquess passed, Cervia gave birth within the year to a son. While it was legally recognized that the son was the deceased marquess' child, it was common knowledge to everyone else that it was actually Karen's child.
For Karen and Cervia, they felt their son could do no wrong and as a result, never corrected him for when he acted out. As a result, their child grew up into an entitled miscreant who would get into fights and cause a lot of raucous without any consequences from the authorities, who feared offending his parents. One night though, this caught up to the young count when he and 2 of his pals tried to jump an elderly man, who quickly proved they were no match for him. While his friends fled, the count drew a knife, wounding the man, only to realize up until that point, the mysterious man had been holding back. Now wounded, the elderly man beat the count up so bad, he was knocked unconscious and soon died from his injuries as neither
magic
nor physicians could heal him.
Discrediting witchers
Incensed at this, both Karen and Cervia used their influence to try and find whoever killed their precious son. However, whoever it was had mysteriously vanished and then a rumor began to spread that it was a witcher. Without actual proof of this, the two began their personal vendetta of attacking and discrediting all witchers. When their petition to King
Eoin
to outlaw witchers was refused on accounts of no proof and that witchers were needed to help settlers, the pair then began to spread lies about the profession, leading to Karen to anonymously write and publish
The Description of a Monstrum or a Witcher
in
1192
, making sure it spread far and wide to scare people. The pair also helped finance and arm the mob that would eventually attack
Kaer Morhen
in
1194
.
