History
In July
1267
, the
Trappers
gang followed
Ciri
's trail to Alvarez Reach, hoping to capture her and claim the reward offered by the prefect. During the same year,
Stefan Skellen
visited the prefect and, under orders from
Imperator
Emhyr var Emreis
, ordered him to continue the search for Ciri, and ruled out the
Rats
as her possible allies, ordering the prefect to hang all seven
gang
members, should they be caught.
