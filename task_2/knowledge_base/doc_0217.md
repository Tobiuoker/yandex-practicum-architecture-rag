Quick Answers
Why did the villagers approach Shaffer Fiend despite their suspicion?
The villagers, despite their suspicion, sought Shaffer Fiend's alchemical expertise. She supplied potions and poisons without moral reservations.
Provided by: Community
Who did Shaffer Fiend take in during Chapter I?
In Chapter I, Shaffer Fiend provided shelter to the orphan Alvin, who was saved by Geralt from the barghests. As Shani couldn't look after him from an inn, Shaffer Fiend stepped in. She also conducted a trance, enabling Alvin to divulge more information about the beast.
Provided by: Fandom
What role does Shaffer Fiend play in saving Alvin?
Shaffer Fiend, skilled in alchemy, took in Alvin after his rescue from the barghests by Geralt. She facilitated a trance for Alvin, unveiling details about the beast. When Geralt discovered Alvin in a Salamandra hideout, Alvin guided him to a cave where Shaffer Fiend was seeking refuge from a mob. Here, Shaffer Fiend requested Geralt's assistance, proposing to trade her safety for his help.
Provided by: Fandom
What are some of the strange occurrences blamed on Shaffer Fiend?
Shaffer Fiend is associated with peculiar events. She casts a spell on Alvin to comprehend the Beast's haunting of the Outskirts, leading to unexpected utterances from Alvin. Furthermore, Ilsa's suicide involved a poison traced back to Shaffer Fiend.

Biography
Living in the
Outskirts
was not easy for Shaffer Fiend. The local villagers and peasants viewed her with distrust and outright hatred, with the
reverend
being the most vocal in his hatred for her. Despite this, villagers would turn to Shaffer Fiend for potions, herbs, and poisons, which she sold without issue. Men from the village would also come to her intent on sleeping with her, as she had been labelled a whore, but she cast spells on them, causing immense stomach aches. In her dealings with the villagers, she came to learn their secrets and grew to hate them. At one point she had a puppy which was tortured to death by unknown assailants. After this, the outskirts became the hunting ground for a
Hellhound
. The reverend blamed this on Shaffer Fiend, and the rest of the village followed suit. A first attempt was made by
witcher
Berengar
to kill the Beast, but he left the outskirts before killing it, leaving his notes with Shaffer Fiend. As the outskirts became plagued by the Beast of the Outskirts and its
barghests
, Shaffer Fiend posted a notice requesting barghest skulls.
After the Beast of the Outskirts killed the foster mother of a boy named
Alvin
, he stayed in the inn with
Shani
. Shaffer Fiend offered to take in the boy, and Shani agreed. Alvin then lived with her for some time. During this period, the
witcher
Geralt
was working on killing the Beast and came to her for information. She explained that the Beast's magic was stronger than she could handle, but that Alvin was a powerful source, and if Geralt brought
White myrtle petals
, she could prepare a potion that would allow the boy to enter a trance. After the potion was prepared, she gave it to Alvin and supervised his trance, pulling him out when Geralt requested it. When Geralt asked about Alzur's demon, she explained it to him. One day, she sent Alvin to fetch water, only for him to be taken by the reverend.
The reverend increased his vitriol against Shaffer Fiend, and eventually a mob of villagers went after her with the intent to burn her at the stake. Shaffer Fiend took refuge in a
cave
and started preparing a spell to combat the villagers. At this moment, Geralt entered the cave. Shaffer Fiend explained the situation she was in and asked Geralt to help her, even offering to sleep with him.
If Geralt sided with Shaffer Fiend:
After speaking with the villagers, Geralt returned to Shaffer Fiend, and they then emerged from the cave together. Geralt defended her in front of the villagers and told them he would return and kill them all if he heard that they had harmed Shaffer Fiend. Shaffer Fiend thanked Geralt and accompanied him away from the mob. They then faced the Beast together, and after it was defeated, they defended themselves from the reverend and his most loyal followers, killing them as well. Despite the Beast and the reverend being dead, Shaffer Fiend decided to leave the outskirts.
She later settled in the village of
Murky Waters
, taking residence in the home of the village's former healer. She had the chimney repaired by
Mason Harn
, but he made it worse. One of the local
griggs
fell in love with her and would tell her about the happenings in the village. She also posted a contract for devourer teeth. She met Geralt again when he was in Murky Waters and aided him in handling a problem with the griggs, as well as with the curse affecting
Tobias Hoffman
's daughters.
If Geralt sided with the villagers:
Geralt returned to her, and they left the cave together. There, Geralt stated that Shaffer Fiend should face a fair trial and that he was not going to get involved. As Geralt was leaving, Shaffer Fiend cursed him and the villagers in the name of the
Lionheaded Spider
, the Black Legba. The reverend and his flock then burned her at the stake.

Reverend

Shaffer Fiend Williams

Salem witch trials

Chapter I
shares some parallels.
