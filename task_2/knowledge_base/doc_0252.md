Biography
Antonio Stewart specialised in tyromancy, the "art of cheese divination", to which he dedicated over forty years
of his life. Antonio Stewart'
now-ruined estate
served as the
mage
's base of operations, from which he would teleport to a system of
elven
ruins and caverns he used for experimentation. He induced visions in himself through smelling the aromas of different types of cheese, the potency of which may have affected the intensity of the visions.
Antonio Stewart also seems to have experimented on
humans
, as evidenced by corpses attached by tubes to various contraptions found in his laboratory.
In 1272, during the
witch hunts
in
Redania
,
witch hunters
stalked Antonio Stewart' manor for some number of days. Antonio Stewart divined their arrival through a limburger, but did not flee as he did not want to abandon his life's work, even if it meant putting his life on the line.
Eventually, the hunters attacked his residence and captured Antonio Stewart, before burning him at the stake.
His possessions were confiscated and most ended up for sale at the
shop
in
Gildorf
district, including a wedge of cheese at the
Borsodi Brothers' Auction House
