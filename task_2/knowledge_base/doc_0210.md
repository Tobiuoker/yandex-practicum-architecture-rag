Abandoned Village
is a burned down village once known as
Hovel
and located in southern
White Orchard
.
History
Hovel had been founded by
Jonathon Powell
and
Volker
, who had had enough of living under White Orchard's
former lord
's reign and, with approval from
Vizima
's court, they were allowed to create a new village that wasn't under Ignatius' rule. Things appeared to go well for the most part until one day Ignatius requested to meet with them in the village. The villagers thought he was a changed man after certain events, but this proved to be untrue and he ended up slaughtering everyone in Hovel.
Jonathon Powell, who'd been hung in the village's well, became a
noonwraith
, preventing anyone from trying to re-settle the area or even draw water from the well she guarded over.
