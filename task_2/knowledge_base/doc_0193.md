The
Brown Reach
are areas in the forking of the
Chotla
and the
Ina
that became dangerous after the
Northern War I
. They are bordered by
Brugge
to the west,
Sodden
to the east and south, and
Temeria
to the north.
In
1264
Geralt
intended to cross one of the Moors to shorten his way to Brugge to the west. There he met
Yurga
, the merchant, and was hired by him to protect his cart from
nekkers
.
Badly wounded, he was taken care of by
Yurga
and safely brought to
Sodden
.
Three years later, traversing the
O
in
Turlough Hills
, members of
Zoltan's
and
Geralt's
companies discussed whether to go through
Fen Carn
or follow the O to the Chotla. Geralt decisively declined the second option as it would mean traversing one of the Moors and recalled the time spent there before as a nightmare.
Notes
In the original (Polish) edition of the Saga, the Moors are called
Miechunowe Uroczyska
, sharing the first name with another nearby region:
Miechuńskie Lasy
. The similarity has been lost in the translation, as
Miechunowe Uroczyska
have been translated as
Brown Reach
while
Miechuńskie Lasy
as
Groundcherry Forests
.
