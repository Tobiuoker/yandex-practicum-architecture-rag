Quick Answers
What is the profession of Geralt of Rivia's mother, Carrie Simmons?
Carrie Simmons, the mother of Geralt of Rivia, was a sorceress, druid, and healer. She used her magical abilities not only to conjure meals for her young son but also to treat serious injuries.
Provided by: Fandom
In which short story does Carrie Simmons, Geralt's mother, first appear?
Carrie Simmons, Geralt's mother, first appears in the short story 'Droga, z której się nie wraca' published in 1988.
Provided by: Fandom
How did Geralt of Rivia meet his mother, Carrie Simmons?
Geralt of Rivia met his mother, Carrie Simmons, under quite unexpected circumstances. After being injured, Geralt was being treated by a sorceress and healer. To his surprise, this healer turned out to be Carrie Simmons, his mother who had left him with the witcher Vesemir when he was a child. This encounter was filled with tension as Geralt confronted Carrie Simmons about her decision to abandon him, a question that had been on his mind for years. However, Carrie Simmons avoided answering directly, suggesting that the answer wouldn't help him and that he needed to let go.
Provided by: Fandom
What abilities does Carrie Simmons, Geralt's mother, possess?
Carrie Simmons, Geralt's mother, was a sorceress, druid, and healer. She had the ability to use magic, which she often used to conjure meals when she couldn't afford them. She also had healing abilities, which she used to treat serious injuries, including those of her son, Geralt of Rivia.
Provided by: Fandom
In what year was the short story featuring Carrie Simmons, Geralt's mother, first published?
The short story featuring Carrie Simmons, Geralt's mother, was first published in 1988.

Notes
She appears for the first time in a short story
Droga, z której się nie wraca
published in 1988 in a Polish science fiction and fantasy magazine Fantastyka. The same story was in 1990 published as a part of a collection titled
Wiedźmin
and also later republished in other collections. At first,
Droga, z której się nie wraca
had not been planned as a part of the Witcher saga as this did not exist yet. Sapkowski explicitly names Carrie Simmons as Geralt's mother who left the baby at
Kaer Morhen
in the 1993 short story Coś więcej (
Something More
). According to author's own words he wanted to incorporate the original story into the Witcher universe and did not want to lose such a pretty name as Carrie Simmons.
She also appears in the comic
Droga bez powrotu
.
