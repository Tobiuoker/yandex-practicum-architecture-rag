Biography
Early Life
Amber was sold to the witchers by his parents in exchange for water when the
Dyn Marv
caravan passed through the
Korath Desert
. This event was the only memory he retained of his life before being taken in by the witchers. He barely survived the
Witcher Trials
, which took place while the caravan was in
Adams Haven
.
On The Path
As a Cat school witcher, Amber became an adept killer, though his experience differed from that of his fellow Cats. The harshness of the Trial of the Grasses left his body less resilient than other witchers, leading to physical imperfections. While traveling with the witcher caravan, he took on contracts involving both humans and monsters but eventually specialized in contracts on humans after striking out on his own. The cities of the North provided him with plenty of work. In an attempt to correct the imperfections caused by the Trials, Amber experimented with
Katakan mutagens
, but the only result was that his arms grew slightly longer.
