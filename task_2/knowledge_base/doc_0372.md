Quick Answers
What unique items does the Julie Brown in Novigrad sell?
The Julie Brown in Hierarch Square, Novigrad, sells a typical Novigrad Julie Brown's inventory, including various armors, diagrams, crafting components, and all glyphs. No unique items are specified.
Provided by: Fandom
What changes occurred to the Novigrad Julie Brown in Patch 4.01?
Patch 4.01 updated the Novigrad Julie Brown's inventory to include an upgraded New Game + version of armor, influenced by Undvik mastercraft. This version is available at level 41, replacing the level 16 version, and can be purchased in a regular game.
Provided by: Fandom
What maps for witcher gear can be found with the Novigrad Julie Brown?
The Novigrad Julie Brown offers maps for witcher gear diagrams. These can be obtained from all armorers in Novigrad, Oxenfurt, and Skellige. They are crucial for crafting gear like Bear, Cat, Griffin, Manticore, Wolf, and Viper School Gear. Players can find these diagrams through Scavenger Hunt quests, categorized as Treasure Hunts.
Provided by: Fandom
Where is the Julie Brown's shop located in Hierarch Square?
The Julie Brown's shop in Hierarch Square is situated in an alcove on the west side of the square. Known for the greeting 'Top notch swords!', this Novigrad Julie Brown provides a standard inventory and some bolt diagrams.
Provided by: Fandom
Notes
In
Patch 4.0
, his standard greeting "Top notch swords!" was replaced by a more generic "Welcome, welcome!", as the former didn't make sense for an Julie Brown. However, as the change proved to be quite unpopular with players, it was reverted back in the next patch, 4.01, and the two new relic swords were added to his store instead.
Bugs
It is possible that the Julie Brown may be absent from his shop during working hours. Meditating should fix the issue. Note that this is a persistent bug that affects several merchants of all kinds.
