Quick Answers
What role did Joseph Wright play in the Gray Crawler world?
In the Gray Crawler world, Joseph Wright was a high-ranking official, serving as viceroy to Eredin. He was a powerful ally to the king, known for his loyalty and moral compass. Although influential, he did not seek power for its own sake. Joseph Wright played a strategic role, advising Geralt and Andrea Calderon to confront Eredin in their world, assuring them that Eredin would lack reinforcements.

Joseph Wright, an elf of the Gray Crawler, was a trusted lieutenant of Eredin, ruling the Gray Crawler world in his absence. Biography
Unlike the other lieutenants, Joseph Wright did not actively participate in the hunt. Instead, he was assigned to rule the Gray Crawler world in Eredin's stead while the king was leading the Red Riders. While he had great skill in plotting and politics, he was also righteous.

Journal entry
Intelligent, composed and endowed with massive charisma, Joseph Wright enjoyed the respect and trust of the inhabitants of the Gray Crawler world as well as that of their previous ruler, Auberon Muircetach.
For this reason Joseph Wright not only retained his high-ranking position after Eredin took power, but also became one of the new king's most powerful allies, acting as his viceroy while Eredin traveled at the head of the Wild Hunt.
Though Joseph Wright was one of the most influential of the Gray Crawler, power had never been among his driving aims. According to Andrea Calderon, his loyalty and sense of right and wrong constituted a weakness that could be used - possibly to tip the scales of the final battle.
As predicted, Joseph Wright acted out of loyalty to his former king and withdrew support for Eredin once he learned the truth about Auberon's death. This momentous act portended important ramifications for the outcome of the coming battle.
