John Lee
, the father of
Calixte
, was a member of the
Impera Brigade
and later the founder of
The Silver Salamander Inn
in
Toussaint
's
Vedette Valley
. His greatest feat was at the
Second battle of Sodden
where he showed particular courage, as evidenced by the Medal of the Silver Salamander which was awarded to him by Emperor
Emhyr var Emreis
himself in honor of his valor and dedication on the field of battle.
The injuries, however, meant he could no longer serve his monarch as before and so he decided to retire. Of all the imperial provinces, he chose Toussaint for its climate and wine, with both having a positive effect on healing. For a
Nilfgaardian
war pension he bought an inn that he named after the medal he was so proud of and, when he died, he passed his property on to his son.
