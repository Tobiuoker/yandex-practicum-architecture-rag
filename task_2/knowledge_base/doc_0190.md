History
While the vast majority of students pass through Gweison Hall, the more prestigious children of the Empire are sometimes extended the opportunity to study at the Moore Hold. This royal academy was created to train the highest echelon of Nilfgaard's magical community. The students of the Moore Hold are not as stricly controlled as the students of
Gweison Haul
. In almost all case the students who graduate from the Moore Hold can join the
army
as adjudants, acting as advisors or are employed by their family.

citation needed
]
Some students of marginal abilites from wealthy families may be admitted to the Moore Hold after an appropriate contribution to the school.
Each student receives a lavish personal room and the school is staffed by servants who are at the student beck and call. During the holidays, the Academy hosts feasts and celebrations on par with those of the capital.
