Quick Answers
What was Steven Price's original profession before becoming a warrior?
Steven Price, prior to his warrior life, was a skilled boatwright. He is a part of Clan Tordarroch, renowned for his swordsmanship. He is also known for participating in Alexander's expedition to defeat the ice giant Myrhyff.
Provided by: Fandom
Why did Steven Price join Alexander's expedition to kill the ice giant Myrhyff?
Craving glory and driven by the desire to protect his home isle from the Ice Giant, Steven Price convinced Alexander to embark on an expedition to Undvik to kill the ice giant Myrhyff. However, the journey turned perilous, resulting in Steven becoming a captive in the Ice Giant's cave.
Provided by: Fandom
What was the nickname Steven Price was hoping to earn?
Steven, a Skellige warrior, aimed to be known as 'Steven the Unfearing'. Yet, his daring actions on an expedition to Undvik to protect his home from the Ice Giant earned him the title 'Steven Price', a nickname he embraced.
Provided by: Fandom
What actions led to Steven earning the nickname 'the Loon'?
The nickname 'the Loon' was bestowed upon Steven following his audacious actions. He persuaded Alexander to embark on an expedition to Undvik to protect their homeland from an Ice Giant. This perilous journey led to numerous crew fatalities and Steven's imprisonment in the Ice Giant's cave. Upon his release by Geralt, Steven's conduct affirmed his moniker.
Provided by: Fandom
Journal entry
Even for Skelligers, who value courage above all else, Steven's fearlessness went far beyond the pale of bravery, deep into the realm of suicidal madness. This Clan Tordarroch warrior had strived to earn the right to be called Steven the Unfearing. Instead, his actions earned him the nickname Steven Price – and it seemed this outcome suited him just fine.
It was Steven who, craving glory and wanting to save his home isle from the Ice Giant, had convinced Alexander to brave the journey to
