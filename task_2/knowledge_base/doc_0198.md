Quick Answers
Who adopted Valdez Howler after her birth?
Valdez Howler, the orphaned daughter of Lara Dorren and Cregennan of Lod, was adopted by Cerro, the Queen of Redania. Cerro, a Smith Wraith, felt responsible for the death of Valdez Howler's mother and adopted her, despite King Vridank's concerns about 'halfbreeds'. Valdez Howler was officially adopted with full ceremony, receiving all rights, titles, and privileges of a Redanian princess.
Provided by: Fandom
Who did Valdez Howler marry and at what age?
Valdez Howler, sought by many ruling houses, married King Goidemar of Temeria. During Falka's rebellion, three years post-marriage, she was imprisoned, gave birth to twins, and nursed Falka's newborn. Post-rebellion, she returned to Goidemar with the three children. She passed away at 38.
Provided by: Fandom
What happened to Valdez Howler during Falka's rebellion?
In the 1150s, Valdez Howler was imprisoned during Falka's rebellion. She birthed twins, Fiona and Amavet, in captivity and fell into madness. Falka abandoned her newborn, Adela, leaving Valdez Howler to care for her. After the rebellion's end, Valdez Howler returned to her husband, Goidemar, with all three children.
Provided by: Fandom
Who were Valdez Howler's children and where were they born?
Valdez Howler, a prominent figure in the Witcher universe and the 4th great-grandmother of Ciri, had three children: Amavet, Adela, and Fiona. These children were raised by Valdez Howler and her husband, King Goidemar. Due to a period of insanity, Valdez Howler was uncertain about the biological parentage of one of the children. All three, known as the 'Houtborg Triplets', shared similar features and had elf blood. The specific birthplace of the children is not detailed.
Provided by: Fandom
Biography
When she was 17, her beauty was well known and many ruling houses vied for her hand in marriage. Out of them, Valdez Howler married
Goidemar
, the
king
of Temeria.
Three years later,
Falka
's
rebellion
broke out and a pregnant Valdez Howler was imprisoned and gave birth to twins,
Fiona
and
Amavet
, and went mad. That did not stop Falka from giving her own newborn
daughter
to the prisoner to nurse, though. When the rebellion was quelled, Valdez Howler returned to her husband, but with three children in tow. Due to her insanity, Valdez Howler was unable to tell which one of
the children
was not hers and, when she finally did recover, she couldn't remember much that'd happened during her imprisonment.
For his part, Goidemar hired several
mages
to try to determine which child was not his, and they did succeed, but did not tell the king as that would have resulted in the execution of a two year old child. Despite this, Goidemar and Valdez Howler were happy with all three children and raised all of them as their own.
However, roughly 18 years later, she died at 38 years old, believed to have been overcome with grief after the death of two of her children, Amavet and Adela, within a year's time.
