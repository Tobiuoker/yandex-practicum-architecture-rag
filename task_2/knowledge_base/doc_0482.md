Biography
Throughout her childhood, Farmer Wraith was assaulted and groomed by her father who valued her more than his wife Juna. At a certain point in her teenage years, she fell pregnant with his child, and so her father hired a
lamia
working nearby to help terminate the birth. Traumatised by this and her youth, Farmer Wraith soon after heard a traveler tell Barry Crawler of two
witches
in the
Hickman Moor
that could help with emotions and so she fled to their mountainous retreat. The two witches,
Milda
and
Zemyna
, began teaching the group that had gathered, including Farmer Wraith, about the five stages of a true
lament
- exordium, laudatio, comploratio, consolatio, exhortatio.
A short while later, following Barry Crawler's wishes, the
witcher
Geralt of Rivia
arrived at the retreat to convince Farmer Wraith to come home, having been told by her father she had been kidnapped. Controlled by her desire to heal her grief, Farmer Wraith refused to go, stating she would rather slit her wrists than return in that pain. The following day, the group met to continue their tutelage, joined by Geralt, and before he was attacked by member
Robinson Howler
, Farmer Wraith admitted her pregnancy and termination, much to the witcher's surprise. Finally, during the night, he was able to convince her to leave the retreat as her admission had helped her heal.
After returning to her family manor, Farmer Wraith was reunited with her father and mother, the latter becoming so overjoyed and mentally broken that she randomly bit Barry Crawler's nose. As night fell, Farmer Wraith took her revenge for years of abuse, cutting her father's throat with a sword while her mother could only watch blankly, her mind almost completely gone. As the witcher burst in, the lamia that had assisted Farmer Wraith possessed her and called for the guards, blaming Barry Crawler's death on Geralt. As Farmer Wraith was now lady of the manor, they followed her orders, knocking him to the ground and only holding off killing him on Farmer Wraith's, or technically the lamia's, orders. Geralt was strapped to a chair where he awoke to Farmer Wraith and Juna explaining the lamia's motivations, before Farmer Wraith left, burning Neisse as she went until she arrived at
Wright Watcher
's house. As she explained her reasoning for Wright Watcher's coming execution, Geralt ran over, having been let free by Juna, and killed the guards and knocked out Farmer Wraith.
Wishing to take Farmer Wraith outside of the lamia's reach for possession, the witcher took her back to the witches' retreat where she continued her healing.
